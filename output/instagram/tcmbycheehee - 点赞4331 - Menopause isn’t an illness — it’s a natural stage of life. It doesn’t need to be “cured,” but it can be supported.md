## Caption

Menopause isn’t an illness — it’s a natural stage of life. It doesn’t need to be “cured,” but it can be supported.

In Western medicine, the focus is often on symptom management — such as hot flashes, night sweats, or mood swings — with options like hormone therapy, antidepressants, or lifestyle advice. These can provide relief, but they may not address the root imbalances.

In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), we look beyond symptoms. Menopause is often linked to Yin deficiency — the body’s cooling, nourishing force becoming depleted over time. Instead of suppressing symptoms, TCM works to restore balance by nourishing Yin and supporting the body’s natural rhythms. 

The goal isn’t to fight against menopause, but to flow with nature and maintain harmony inside and out.
