## Script

There's one organ in your body that won't wake up if you skip breakfast. In Chinese medicine, breakfast isn't just about eating. It's about turning on your body's systems for the day. Your gallbladder works through the night and the morning, it passes that job to your digestive system. But if you skip breakfast, that on switch never gets pressed. That can make you feel sluggish, tired, and out of sync all day long. Eating three meals a day, starting with breakfast, helps your organs stay in rhythm, keeps your energy steady, and supports your body's natural flow. So fuel your body three times a day and never skip the most important one.
