## Script

Did you know one of the most powerful Chinese healing ideas actually came from ancient war strategy? In ancient China, there's something called the 36 stratagems. In Chinese, 三十六计. It's a collection of short strategies used in war and politics. Not about fighting harder, but about winning smarter. And one of those strategies is called besiege Wei to rescue Zhao. Wei Wei救赵. The idea is simple. Don't attack the problem directly. Fix it by going somewhere else. In ancient warfare, instead of attacking a heavily defended city, you attack a target the enemy can't ignore, forcing them to retreat. Now here's where it gets interesting. That's exactly how traditional Chinese medicine thinks. You have headaches? TCM might treat your liver. You can't sleep? We don't just look at your brain. We look at your heart, kidneys, your whole system. Because in Chinese medicine, the symptom isn't always the real problem. So if you've been trying to fix the same issue over and over, but it keeps coming back, maybe you're attacking the wrong place. In Chinese medicine, real healing happens when you treat the root, not just the surface.
