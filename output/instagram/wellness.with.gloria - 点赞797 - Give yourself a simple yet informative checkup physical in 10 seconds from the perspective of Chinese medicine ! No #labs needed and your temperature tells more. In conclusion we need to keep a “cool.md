## Script

10-second checkup with your bare hands. Before we start, make sure you are in room temperature with your hands naturally warm. If your hands are always cold, you are already out of the game. Step 1, place your hand on your forehead. It's a good sign when your forehead is a little bit cooler than your hand. Step 2, place your hand on your lower ab. If it's warm here, you are doing better than 90% of people. Especially for women, this is the key sign of your gyno health. Now comes the most important last step, your feet. There was a well-known TCM master who once lived in the US. He was renowned for treating the most severe illnesses like cancer or kidney failure. He said, if I want to know if someone still has a chance, I simply bend down and touch their feet. If the feet are cold, it's very difficult for me to save them. In TCM, serious illnesses come down to one thing. The body is just too cold. And cold starts from the feet. So that's why soaking our feet has become a Chinese culture for centuries.
