I’m a Chinese Medicine student, and I stand strong by Chinese Medicine because I know that it works 🫶

Chinese Medicine has been around since thousands of years ago, when humans had nothing other than what they could get from Mother Nature to treat their illness.

After countless trials and errors, generations of Chinese Medicine doctors, now known as practitioners, finally founded a complete system with unique characteristics, including but not limited to pulse taking, Tongue diagnosis, acupuncture, cupping, GuaSha, Chinese Herbal Medicine, and much more.

After thousands of years with written records of both successful and failed clinical case studies, a strong theoretical foundation, and the growing scientific evidence gathered from modern clinical studies (available online), Chinese Medicine is now one of the strongest traditional and complementary medical systems in the world, helping patients worldwide cope with their acute and chronic illnesses.
