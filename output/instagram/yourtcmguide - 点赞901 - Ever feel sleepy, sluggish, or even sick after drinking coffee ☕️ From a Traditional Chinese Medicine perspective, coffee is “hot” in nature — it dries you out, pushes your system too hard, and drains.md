Ever drink coffee and end up feeling sleepy, sluggish, or even sick instead of energized? Traditional Chinese medicine actually has a clear explanation for this. This happens to me too. Sometimes after coffee, I don't feel awake at all. I feel heavy, drained, or even unwell. In Chinese medicine, your digestion is like the engine that powers your whole body. It's meant to gently turn food and drink into steady energy. Coffee is considered hot in nature, and that doesn't mean the temperature in your cup. Even iced coffee is considered hot because of how it acts inside your body. It speeds you up, dries you out, and pushes your system harder than it can handle. At first, you might feel a spark, but if your energy is already low, coffee burns through what little you have left. That's why instead of feeling sharp, you crash, or feel sick, jittery, or weighed down. It's like borrowing from tomorrow's energy and ending up in debt today. So if coffee leaves you sleepy, sluggish, or sick, your body is not failing. It's giving you a signal. From a TCM perspective, what really helps is gentle support. Warm teas, simple nourishing foods, or even rest. That way, you get energy that lasts without the crash.
