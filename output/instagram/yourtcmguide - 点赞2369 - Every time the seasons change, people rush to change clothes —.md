## Caption

Every time the seasons change, people rush to change clothes —
but Chinese medicine says that’s actually the worst thing you can do.

There’s an old saying, “春捂秋冻,” which means:
stay warm a little longer in spring,
and stay cool a little longer in fall.

In spring, your body’s warmth is still waking up — dressing too light too soon lets cold sneak in, causing fatigue or seasonal colds.
In fall, your body still holds leftover summer heat — if you bundle up too early, you trap that heat inside and weaken your immune system.

So remember: balance is everything.
Warm a bit longer in spring, cool a bit longer in fall — and your body will thank you. 🌿

## Script

If you put away your jacket too early in spring, or grab it too fast in fall, you're doing the opposite of what your body actually needs, and Chinese medicine has the perfect explanation why. There's an old Chinese saying, 春午秋冬, which means keep warm in spring and stay cool in fall. In spring, your body's warmth, your inner fire, is still waking up after winter. If you expose yourself to cold too soon, it can sneak in and weaken your energy, leading to fatigue, allergies, even colds. So you should woo, or stay covered a bit longer. But in fall, it's the opposite. Your body still carries leftover heat from the summer. If you bundle up too quickly, you trap that heat inside, and your body doesn't get a chance to adjust to the cooler air. That's why a little dong, or light cooling, actually strengthens your immunity. So remember, stay warm a little longer in spring, and don't rush to wear heavy clothes in fall.
