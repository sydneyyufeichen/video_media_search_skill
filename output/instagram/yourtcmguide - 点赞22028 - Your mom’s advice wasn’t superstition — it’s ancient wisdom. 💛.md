## Script

Can you eat bananas during your period? Your mom might say no, and she's actually right. In traditional Chinese medicine, bananas are cold in nature and damp producing. That means they can slow your blood flow and weaken digestion, especially when your body's already more delicate during your period. Think about it like this. When you're on your period, your body's trying to push blood and energy downward. You need warmth to help that movement. But cold foods like bananas, ice drinks, and smoothies act like pouring cold water on that flow. Everything slows down. That's when cramps hit harder, bloating feels worse, or your period feels heavier or late. So what should you eat instead? Warm foods that comfort and support your body, like ginger tea, red dates, or even a simple bowl of warm congee. They help keep your womb cozy and your digestion strong, so your body can recover smoothly. So the next time your mom says nobody bananas during your period, she's not being superstitious. She's being wise. Keep your womb warm, and you'll feel the difference.
