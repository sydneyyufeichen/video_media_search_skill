## Script

If you fix only one digestion habit, make it this. Drink warm or hot water, not ice water. In Chinese medicine, digestion works like a fire, and cold water puts that fire out. Every time you drink ice water, your stomach has to spend energy just to warm it up, energy that should have gone to breaking down food. That's why so many people feel bloated, heavy, or tired after meals, even when they eat clean. The food isn't the problem. Your digestion is working at the wrong temperature. And when I say warm water, I don't mean warm water straight from the tap. In China, we boil the water first, then let it cool until it's warm and comfortable to drink. Boiling isn't just for safety. In Chinese medicine, boiling makes water easier for your body to absorb and use. Cold or raw water is harder on a weak digestive system. Aim for about 40 to 60 degrees Celsius or 105 to 140 degrees Fahrenheit. Warm, not burning. And this doesn't mean boiling hot water all day. Just avoid ice cold drinks, especially with an empty stomach or with meals. Warm supports digestion. Cold forces your body to fight first. Try this for seven days. Your body will tell you the difference.
