In this second episode, I introduced the acupuncture point AnMian, a favorite point for insomnia(trouble sleeping). About 1 in 10 people have at least a moderate level of insomnia. A commonly used point to treat insomnia is “Anmian”, which translates into “Peaceful Sleep”. 

In Chinese Medicine, when you are unable to sleep, it is considered that your “spirit is not housed properly” and this acupuncture point can help to settle it again by “calming the spirit”. You can use pressure on this point to create this effect. 

To find it, run your finger back half an inch from behind your earlobe (you’ll feel a small depression), and massage gently in small circles. Do this for 1-3 minutes. You can do it yourself or do it for someone else. You should have a deep aching feeling and a feeling of relaxation. Let me know if it works tomorrow🥰
