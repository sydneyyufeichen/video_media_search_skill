Cold yogurt feels healthy… but in Chinese medicine, it may be doing the opposite.

In TCM, digestion needs warmth to work properly. When the first thing you eat is cold, your body has to spend energy reheating instead of digesting — and that can show up as bloating, brain fog, low energy, or loose stools.

Try warming up your breakfast for 7 days and see how your body feels.

## Script

Chinese medicine hates cold yogurt for breakfast. Let me explain why. In traditional Chinese medicine, yogurt is considered cold and damp. Cold slows digestion, damp creates heaviness and bloating. In Chinese medicine, we say your stomach is like a cooking pot, and in the morning, that pot is just starting to warm up. So if the first thing you put in is something cold like yogurt, it's like pouring cold water into the pot. The temperature drops, and now the fire has to work harder just to reheat instead of actually cooking the food. That's when digestion slows down. That's also why traditionally, Chinese breakfasts are warm and cooked. Things like congee, egg, soup, or noodles. The goal is to support digestion, not shock it first thing in the morning. When digestion stays weak, people start noticing bloating, brain fog, loose stools, low energy after eating, or stubborn weight gain. Some people do fine with yogurt, and that's okay. But if you already have digestion issues, cold yogurt in the morning often makes things worse. Try warming up the pot instead. Cook breakfast, warm foods, or even just warm water first thing. Do it for one week and see how your body feels.
