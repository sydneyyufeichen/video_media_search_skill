Hey guys, I'm going to tell you why everyone in China starts eating bitter melon in the summer, and so should you. And no, it's not some new wellness trend. Chinese people have been eating bitter melon for hundreds of years. The moment summer arrives, you'll see bitter melon everywhere in China. But why? In traditional Chinese medicine, one of the five flavors is bitter, and bitter is believed to help clear heat and cool the body. During the summer, many people experience what Chinese medicine calls summer heat. You might notice more mouth ulcers, acne, irritability, or a poor appetite. That's why bitter foods become much more common this time of year. Bitter melon is one of the most popular choices because it's naturally bitter and considered especially cooling. Modern science supports some of its benefits too. Bitter melon is rich in antioxidants and vitamin C, and studies suggest it may help support healthy blood sugar levels. In China, it's commonly stir-fried with eggs or beef, added to soups, or simply sauteed with garlic. So the next time you see bitter melon at the grocery store, don't avoid it because of the taste. Sometimes that bitter flavor is exactly what your body needs during the summer.
