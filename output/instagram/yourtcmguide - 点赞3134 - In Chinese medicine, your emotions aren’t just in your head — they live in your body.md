## Script

Do you know why, in Chinese medicine, we say too much joy can actually harm your body? To understand this, you need to know one thing. In traditional Chinese medicine, your emotions are directly tied to your organs. Your heart is linked to joy, your liver to stress and anger, your spleen to overthinking, your lungs to grief, and your kidneys to fear. So your emotions aren't just in your head, they live in your body. And it goes both ways. Your emotions can affect your organs, but your organ health can also affect how you feel. Now here is where it gets interesting. In TCM, joy is connected to your heart. But too much excitement, too much stimulation, can throw your heart out of balance. Think about your day, constant scrolling, notifications, short videos, always chasing the next dopamine hit. That's not calm joy, that's overstimulation. And in TCM, that kind of excess joy scatters your shin, which is your mind and spirit. So instead of feeling calm, you feel wired but tired. You can't focus, your sleep gets worse, your mind won't slow down. It's not just stress, your heart can't settle. So the goal isn't to chase more happiness, it's to protect your calm. Because if your mind never settles, your body never recovers. And no amount of stimulation can ever replace true balance.
