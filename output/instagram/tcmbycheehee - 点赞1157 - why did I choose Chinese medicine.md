why did I choose Chinese medicine? 

Twice in my life, I’ve seen my grandparent pass away right before my eyes and felt so helpless that there was nothing I could do at the time. 

I told myself that I had to improve and learn something to help the rest of my family live healthier lives so we can spend more happy times together.

That’s why I chose to learn Chinese Medicine, to help my family. Now, when I see patients in pain, it hurts me as if they are my own family. 

When I see patients suffering from an illness I can’t help with, it pushes me to study harder and polish my skills. I wish to become a Chinese Medicine practitioner capable of helping all my patients recover from their illness.

May everyone be healthy and happy 🫶
