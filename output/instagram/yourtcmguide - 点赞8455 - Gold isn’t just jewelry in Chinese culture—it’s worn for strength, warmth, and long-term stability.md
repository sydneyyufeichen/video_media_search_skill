## Script

Have you ever wondered why so many women in China wear gold, and why it's about way more than just value? From a traditional Chinese medicine perspective, gold is considered warming, strengthening, and stabilizing. In TCM, women are more prone to deficiencies over time, especially qi and blood, due to stress, menstruation, pregnancy, and aging. Gold is believed to support vitality, strengthen the body's core energy, and promote long-term resilience. That's why gold is traditionally worn close to the body, on the neck near the chest, on the wrists where circulation and qi flow are strong. The idea is that gold gently supports your yang energy, your warmth, strength, and life force, through constant contact. From a feng shui perspective, gold represents abundance, stability, and protection. It's associated with wealth that lasts, not quick luck, but steady prosperity and security. That's why gold is often given at major life moments, birthdays, weddings, childbirth, as a symbol of long-term blessing. So in Chinese culture, gold isn't about showing off, it's about strength, protection, and stability over a lifetime.
