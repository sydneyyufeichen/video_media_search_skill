Do you know what it means if your period blood is brown instead of red? Brown bleeding usually shows up right before your flow starts, at the end, or sometimes throughout the whole period. And while some doctors may say it's normal if your tests look fine, let's be honest, brown blood is not normal in common sense. In Chinese medicine, brown bleeding usually points to chronic inflammation in the uterus. It's usually your body's way of saying something isn't working right. Where does that come from? Most often from lifestyle stress. For example, constant anxiety or depression, eating too much processed, heavy, salty, spicy, or sweet food, drinking soda, alcohol, or lots of cold drinks, poor sleep or staying up too late, too much, too little, or exercising at the wrong time. All of these throw your hormones off balance. Once your hormones get out of rhythm, your immune system gets irritated and inflammation shows up, including in your uterus, causing brown bleeding. So if you notice this, don't ignore it. Start by working on your lifestyle. Eat cleaner, get enough sleep, and move your body at the right time. And if it doesn't improve, consider seeing a holistic doctor for deeper support. This is your body's signal. If you want to learn how to reset it, follow along.
