## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say the best time for cardio is between 5 and 7 a.m.? In traditional Chinese medicine, your body runs on a 24-hour organ clock, where different organs are most active at different times of day. From 5 to 7 a.m., your large intestine is most active. This is when your body is naturally clearing waste and waking up circulation. Doing cardio during this window, walking, jogging, cycling, helps your body move energy, improve circulation, and fully wake up your system. That's why people who exercise early often feel more energized and mentally clear throughout the day. From 7 a.m. to 9 a.m., your stomach becomes most active. This is when your body wants to focus on digesting food and absorbing nutrients, not heavy exercise. So intense cardio later in the morning can actually work against your body's natural rhythm. And if you push hard cardio in the evening, it can raise stress hormones, make your body more alert, and even interfere with sleep. So here's a simple rule from Chinese medicine. If you want to do cardio, do it early, ideally between 5 and 7 a.m. Work with your body's clock, not against it.
