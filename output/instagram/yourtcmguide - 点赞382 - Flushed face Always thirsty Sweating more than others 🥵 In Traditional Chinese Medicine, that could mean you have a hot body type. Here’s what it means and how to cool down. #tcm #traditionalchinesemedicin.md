## Script

Do you flush easily, feel thirsty all the time, sweat more often than others, or get irritated for no reason? In traditional Chinese medicine, that's called a hot body type. It means that your body is holding on to more heat than it should, which can make it harder to sleep and leave you feeling restless. It can come from eating a lot of spicy or fried food, drinking alcohol too often, or spending too much time in hot environments. To cool down, eat cucumber, watermelon, and mint. Drink chrysanthemum tea, and cut back on spicy and greasy foods for a while. If you want to know the opposite, called the cold body type, check out my video from yesterday.
