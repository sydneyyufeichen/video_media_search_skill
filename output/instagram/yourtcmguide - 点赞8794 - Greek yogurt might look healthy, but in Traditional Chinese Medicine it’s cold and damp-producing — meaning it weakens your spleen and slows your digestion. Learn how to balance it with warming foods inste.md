## Script

How does traditional Chinese medicine feel about Greek yogurt? Well, it's a double-edged sword. There's some nutrition in it, sure, but from a TCM perspective, it's also one of those foods that can quietly mess with your digestion. See, Greek yogurt is considered cold and damp-producing. And in TCM, cold doesn't just mean temperature. It means it slows down your body's internal metabolism, like pouring ice water on your digestive fire. When that happens, your spleen and stomach tea, which power digestion and energy, get weaker. That's when you start to feel bloated, tired, or heavy after eating. And because it's dairy, it also creates phlegm, that sticky feeling in your throat, sinus congestion, or even acne breakouts for some people. So if you're someone who gets bloated easily, or you constantly deal with mucus or sinus issues, Chinese medicine would say, maybe skip it. But if you love Greek yogurt and don't want to give it up, here's the TCM hack. Let it sit until it's at room temperature, and pair it with warming, spleen-friendly foods, like stewed pears, cooked apples, walnuts, or goji berries. That combo balances out the cold energy and makes it much easier for your body to handle.
