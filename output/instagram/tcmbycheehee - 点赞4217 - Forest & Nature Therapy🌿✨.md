## Caption

Forest & Nature Therapy🌿✨

In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), we believe in 天人合一—the harmony between humans and nature. Just as the body follows natural rhythms, so should we. 

Spending time in the forest isn’t just relaxing; it’s a way to reconnect with the energy of the earth and restore balance within.

The fresh air nourishes 肺 (Lungs), the vibrant greenery soothes 肝 (Liver), and the stillness calms 心 (Heart). Walking barefoot on the ground can even strengthen 肾 (Kidneys), rooting us like a strong tree.

Nature isn’t separate from us—we are part of it. When we align with its flow, true healing begins. 🍃🌏

Have you ever felt refreshed after a walk in nature? That’s the power of oneness. Let’s embrace it. 🌱
