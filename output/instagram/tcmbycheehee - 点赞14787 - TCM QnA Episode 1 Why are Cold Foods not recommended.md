## Script

Why are cold foods bad for you in Chinese medicine? Now, I tell a lot of my patients to not take cold foods or cold drinks, especially when they're taking herbal medicine. And this is because the cold foods and cold drinks, they have a constricting property. Now, in Chinese medicine, our digestive system, which is the spleen and the stomach, both of these organs, they work like a slow cooker that steams and transforms our food into energy that we can use, which are the qi and blood. Cold foods like cold drinks, ice cream, raw salads from the fridge, all of these are like pouring cold water into that boiling cooking pot. The fire, the digestive yang in our digestive system, has to work harder to warm everything up before it can break it down. So over time, this weakens your digestive fire, leading to symptoms like bloating, loose stools, fatigue, and even poor circulation. In Chinese medicine terms, cold foods, they hurt our yang energy, which is that inner fire. It slows down our qi movement, and it creates dampness. So that's why cold foods are not recommended in Chinese medicine.
