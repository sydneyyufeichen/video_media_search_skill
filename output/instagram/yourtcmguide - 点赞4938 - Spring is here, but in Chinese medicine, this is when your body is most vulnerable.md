Spring is here, the weather is getting warmer, so why does Chinese medicine say you shouldn't dress lighter yet? There's a famous phrase, 春午秋冬. It means, bundle up in spring, tolerate cold in autumn. And no, it's not just tradition. In Chinese medicine, spring is when your yang energy starts rising after winter. Your body feels warmer, but it's actually still rebuilding. At the same time, spring weather is unstable. Warm one day, cold the next. Here's the key. As your body warms up, your pores open. That makes it much easier for cold and wind to enter your body. That's why so many people get sick in spring. Colds, allergies, fatigue, even joint pain. It's not random. It's because you expose your body too early. So what should you actually do? Don't rush into t-shirts just because it's sunny. Keep your neck, lower back, and ankles covered. Those are the easiest entry points for cold. Layer your clothes so you can adjust throughout the day. And if you do feel chilled, drink something warm like ginger tea to push the cold out. So the next time it hits 60 degrees and you want to dress like summer, pause for a second.
