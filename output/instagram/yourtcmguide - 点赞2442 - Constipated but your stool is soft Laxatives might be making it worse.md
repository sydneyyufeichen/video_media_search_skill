## Script

If you're constipated or your stool is already soft, stop using laxatives. You might be making it worse. In traditional Chinese medicine, not all constipation is the same, and there's one type most people completely misunderstand. Your stool is soft, but it's still hard to pass. You feel bloated, stuck, uncomfortable. Think of your stomach like a cooking pot. If the fire is too weak, food doesn't get properly transformed or moved. So even if the stool is soft, your body doesn't have enough energy to push it out. But here's the mistake. People take laxatives. But laxatives force movement. They don't fix the weakness. In TCM, overusing laxatives can weaken your gut even more, making the problem worse. So what's causing this? Usually too much cold and raw food. Salads, smoothies, ice drinks. These put out your digestive fire. What to do instead? Warm your system back up. Add ginger. In cooking, raw, or boil it in hot water and drink it. Switch to warm meals and drink hot water. Sometimes the solution isn't to push harder. It's to rebuild your strength first. Follow for more Chinese medicine tips.
