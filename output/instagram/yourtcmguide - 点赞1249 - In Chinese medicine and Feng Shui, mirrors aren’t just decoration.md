## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine and in feng shui we say you should never have a mirror facing your bed? Let me explain. In feng shui, mirrors are believed to reflect and move energy, and during the day, that's considered a good thing. Mirrors can brighten a space, expand a room, and help energy circulate. But at night, it's different, because in traditional Chinese medicine, sleep is when your body shifts from yang to yin, your energy turns inward, and your body is trying to slow down, restore itself, and recharge. But a mirror facing the bed is believed to bounce energy back at you all night long instead of letting your nervous system fully settle. There's also an old Chinese belief that mirrors can disturb the spirit during sleep, especially if you can see your reflection while lying in bed. And honestly, even outside of feng shui, it kind of makes sense. Bedrooms usually feel best when they're visually calm and quiet. But mirrors add light, movement, and reflection into the space, which can make some people feel more alert or unsettled at night, especially in the dark. So if you have a mirror facing your bed, try covering it up tonight and see how you sleep. You might be surprised how much calmer your room feels.
