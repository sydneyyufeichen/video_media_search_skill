In Chinese medicine, your body runs on something called the organ clock, and there are two critical times at night you can't miss. From 11 p.m. to 1 a.m., your gallbladder is most active. This is when your body resets decision-making and mental clarity. So if you're overthinking, second-guessing yourself, or feeling mentally stuck, it might not just be stress. It might be your sleep timing. Then from 1 a.m. to 3 a.m., your liver takes over. Your liver regulates emotions, moves energy, and stores blood. So if you feel tired, irritable, or emotionally off for no reason, this is usually where the problem starts. The problem is modern life is working against you. Phones, lights, scrolling at night, or doing things humans were never designed to do. Before technology, people slept when it got dark. Now we stay up past midnight, and your body pays the price. And look, if you work night shifts, I get it. Changing schedules is not always an option. Just focus on getting enough sleep when you can, but understand it's not optimal for your body. If you can adjust your schedule, start small. Move your bedtime 30 minutes earlier each night. Fix your timing, and your energy, mood, and sleep will change fast.
