## Caption

I miss them🤍
.
It happened twice. I saw my own grandparents pass away in front of me. My grandfather was a healthy and strong old man. After he went for a medical checkup one day, he was suggested to be admitted to the ICU and he soon passed away due to a worsening lung condition. I was ten years old that year.

A few years ago, my grandmother passed away at home when she was recovering from a recent knee surgery. I was already learning Chinese Medicine at the time, but I wasn’t skilled enough to help her cope with her condition. Even when she passed away, there was nothing I could do as a student. That was really painful time when I blamed myself for not learning hard enough to help her. Because maybe, just maybe if I studied harder, I could’ve helped her.

Since then I have decided to work hard in my studies and practice, so that I can find the answer to living a healthy life. 

I wish to share the little knowledge that I know to help more people learn to live healthier lives. Prevention is better than cure, and I believe in practicing healthy habits everyday to prevent illness.

This is my story. If you like to learn more about Chinese Medicine and how to use it to live a healthier life, consider following me @tcmbycheehee💛
