## Script

If you have indigestion, I'm going to share one of the simplest traditional Chinese medicine tricks, and it's probably already in your kitchen. Eat raw peanuts, but there's a right way to do it. In Chinese medicine, peanuts are known to gently support the stomach and help it receive and break down food better. Here's how to use them. Take 10 to 20 high-quality raw peanuts a day, and here's the key. You have to chew them extremely fine before you swallow. Not just a few bites. Chew until they almost turn to a paste in your mouth. When you do it right, something interesting happens. At first, they feel slightly nutty and dry, but as you keep chewing, they become smooth, creamy, almost milky. It feels like your mouth is naturally moistening, like your body is waking up your digestion before the food even hits your stomach. When you swallow, it goes down soft and warm, not heavy. That's exactly what your stomach likes. They have to be raw, not roasted, not salted, and high quality, because in Chinese medicine, too much heat from roasting can actually make digestion worse. It's simple, but if your digestion feels off, bloated, or sluggish, try this for a few days and see how your body responds. Sometimes the most basic foods are the most powerful medicine.
