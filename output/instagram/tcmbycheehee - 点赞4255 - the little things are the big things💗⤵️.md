## Script

I never really liked kids, but after volunteering at a refugee school today, my heart has completely changed. Today reminded me how important it is to connect with others and embrace those simple happy moments. Sometimes, we get so focused on chasing big dreams, like getting good grades or making lots of money, that we forget what truly matters. It's the small acts of kindness, a smile, a gift, or just being there for someone that can really make a huge difference in their lives. These kids taught me that having fun is essential and happiness is actually very simple. Whether it's sharing a meal, drawing together, or just playing, these moments can change lives. So if you ever get caught up in the hustle for bigger things, remember, the secret to happiness is actually in appreciating the little things.
