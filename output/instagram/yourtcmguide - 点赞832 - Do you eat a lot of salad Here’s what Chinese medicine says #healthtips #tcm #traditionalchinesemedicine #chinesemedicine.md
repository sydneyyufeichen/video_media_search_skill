## Script

Do you eat a lot of salad? In Chinese medicine, salad is hard to digest. Raw vegetables are cold and damp in nature. That's what makes them tough on your digestion, especially if you feel bloated, cold, or tired after your meals. Chinese medicine teaches that your stomach needs warmth to function properly. So instead of eating raw salads every day, try eating lightly cooked veggies such as stirred fried greens or warm soups.
