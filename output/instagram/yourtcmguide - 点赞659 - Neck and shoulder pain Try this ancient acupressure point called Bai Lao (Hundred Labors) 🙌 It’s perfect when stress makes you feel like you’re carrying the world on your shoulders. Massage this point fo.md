## Script

Do you feel like you're carrying the weight of the world on your shoulders? Let me show you an acupressure point called Bai Lao, which translates to 100 labors in Chinese. Take your fingers and curl them around the back of your neck until they meet in the center. From there, move about one inch outward to either side, and that's where the point is. You might feel some tightness or tenderness here. Avoid pressing directly on the spine. You can press both sides at once, or one at a time. Try holding steady pressure or gently rub in circles. Back and forth, or up and down along the muscle. You can even use your whole hand for a broader massage. Hold or massage this point for about 30 seconds. It's amazing for releasing neck and shoulder tension. Try it now and let me know if you feel the difference.
