## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we put tiny seeds like these on the ear to treat problems throughout the body? Here's the idea behind it. It's called ear seeding, and according to traditional Chinese medicine, the ear is believed to be a microsystem of the entire body. That means different parts of the ear correspond to different organs or different body regions. When a specific point is stimulated, it's believed to influence the corresponding part of the body and help restore the smooth flow of qi. That's why Chinese medicine practitioners use ear seeds. Instead of acupuncture needles, tiny seeds or metal beads are placed on specific acupressure points and held in place with adhesive tape. Then throughout the day, you gently press on them to repeatedly stimulate those points. Think of it as acupuncture without the needles. Chinese medicine practitioners have used ear seeding as one approach to address concerns like stress, poor sleep, digestion, menstrual discomfort, muscle tension, and a wide range of issues.
