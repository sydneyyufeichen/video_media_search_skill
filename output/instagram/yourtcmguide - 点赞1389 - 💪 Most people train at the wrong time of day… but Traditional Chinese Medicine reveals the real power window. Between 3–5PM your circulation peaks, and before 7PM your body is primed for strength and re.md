Here's a Chinese medicine secret. If you're lifting weights after 5 p.m., you're actually working against your body. In traditional Chinese medicine, 3 to 5 p.m. is bladder time. That's when your circulation and energy peak, your joints are looser, and you're less likely to get injured. That makes it the perfect time for strength training. Before 5 p.m. Train earlier and eat dinner afterwards to refuel. That way, you're working with your body's natural rhythm, not against it.
