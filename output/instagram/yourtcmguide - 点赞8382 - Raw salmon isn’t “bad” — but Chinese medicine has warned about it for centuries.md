## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine, raw salmon is one of the riskiest foods for your body? You've probably heard this before. One square inch of salmon can carry up to 10,000 parasite eggs. And no, this isn't just farmed fish, wild salmon too. So why salmon? From a Western lens, it's because salmon is high fat, and parasites love fat. But in Chinese medicine, we've been saying something similar for thousands of years, just in a different way. In TCM, salmon is considered very rich, very oily, and very damp-producing. And here's the key rule. Where there is dampness, parasites thrive. Dampness is heavy, sticky energy. When too much of it builds up in your body, it creates the perfect environment for unwanted things to build up. Parasites, inflammation, sluggish digestion. That's why foods that are raw, cold, oily, and fatty, like sashimi, pokeballs, or raw salmon, are considered especially taxing on your digestive system. This is also why Chinese medicine prefers cooked fish, with warming spices like ginger and scallion, to protect your digestion and prevent damp buildup. If you eat salmon, cook it well. Your gut will thank you.
