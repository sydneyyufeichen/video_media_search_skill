## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say that your period blood is like a health report card? Bright red and smooth? That's healthy. It shows that your blood and energy are flowing freely. If it's pale and watery, that points to blood deficiency, often linked with fatigue, bloating, and light periods. Dark red with clots usually means stagnation or heat, the reason behind painful cramps or heavy bleeding. And if it's purplish, that's blood stasis, often tied to severe cramps or conditions like fibroids. This is your body signal. If you want to learn how to reset it, follow along.
