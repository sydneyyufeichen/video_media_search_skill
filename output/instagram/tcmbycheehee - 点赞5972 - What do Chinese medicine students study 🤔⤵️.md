What do Chinese medicine students study? 🤔⤵️

Here’s a glimpse into the ancient wisdom and practices you’ll explore as a Chinese medicine student✨

1. Acupuncture: Beyond the needles lies a practice of balance, stimulating specific points to unblock Qi energy. Fun fact: Acupuncture points are gateways to your body’s energy flow!

2. Tai Chi: Not just a martial art, but a moving meditation. The slow, graceful movements enhance balance, focus, and calm. Benefits include stress reduction and improved mental well-being.

3. Cupping: Those intriguing marks are signs of toxins being drawn out! This therapy uses suction to promote healing, ease muscle tension, and improve circulation.

4. Massage (Tui Na): More than relaxation, Tui Na is a therapeutic touch that harmonizes Qi, addresses specific ailments, and supports overall health.

5. Pulse Diagnosis: A touch of the wrist, a world of knowledge. This technique reveals the state of your internal organs and Qi flow, guiding personalized treatment plans.

6. Moxibustion: Warmth that heals. Burning moxa (dried mugwort) near the skin stimulates acupuncture points, enhancing vitality and immune response.

Each of these practices offers unique insights into health, emphasizing prevention, and natural harmony. Learning Chinese medicine is really fun and exciting, what I love most about it is you can apply it to improve your own health and treat your own family🥰
