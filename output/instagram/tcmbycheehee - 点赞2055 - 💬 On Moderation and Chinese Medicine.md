## Script

So one of the key principles in Chinese medicine is moderation. So the Chinese word for traditional Chinese medicine is 中一. These are the two characters, 中 and 一. 一 means medicine, and 中 has several meanings. The first meaning is China. It's from China. The second meaning is Chinese. The third meaning, it's the center. So it focuses on the center and digestion. That's why we say that in Chinese medicine, 脾为后天之本. After you're born, we need to support the digestion. Because by supporting the digestion, it is the foundation for a healthy life. That is the third meaning of 中. And the fourth meaning of 中 is moderation. Whatever you do, you have to do it in moderation. Eating in moderation. Don't eat too much, don't eat too little. Even exercising in moderation. That is the definition of 中 in Chinese medicine. That's why we highly recommend practicing moderation in daily life.
