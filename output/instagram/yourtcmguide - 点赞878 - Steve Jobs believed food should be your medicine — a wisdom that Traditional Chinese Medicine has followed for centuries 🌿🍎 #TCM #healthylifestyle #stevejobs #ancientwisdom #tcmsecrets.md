## Script

Steve Jobs once said, eat your food as medicine, or one day you'll eat medicine as your food. That quote sounds poetic, but it's also deeply practical. And it's exactly what traditional Chinese medicine has believed for thousands of years. In TCM, food isn't just fuel, it's your first line of defense. Every bite you take is either helping your body stay in balance or slowly pushing it off track. Warm cooked meals protect your digestion, like gently tending a fire. Cooling foods soothe the body when it's overheated or inflamed. Sour flavors strengthen the liver, bitter flavors clear toxins, and sweet flavors rebuild energy when you're run down. That's why in China, every season has its own healing foods, because your body's needs change with the weather. But when we eat processed foods, skip meals, or load up on sugar, we confuse the body. And that confusion eventually leads to sickness. By then, food can't help us anymore, only medicine can. So yes, Steve Jobs was right. Eat your food as medicine before medicine becomes your food.
