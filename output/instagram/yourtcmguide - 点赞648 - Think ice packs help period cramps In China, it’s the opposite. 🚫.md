News flash! In China, women almost never use ice packs for period cramps. Here's what they do instead. In traditional Chinese medicine, cramps are seen as cold, causing the blood to contract and stagnate. That slow circulation makes blood flow less smoothly and can even make the cramps come back even stronger. That's why Chinese women are taught to avoid cold, not just during their period, but all month long. No cold drinks, no icy foods, and definitely no ice packs. Instead, they reach for warmth. A hot water bottle, a heating pad, or even sipping warm ginger tea helps relax the muscles, open up circulation, and ease pain in a way that lasts longer. So while in the West, an ice pack feels like the go-to, in China, it's the opposite. Warmth is medicine.
