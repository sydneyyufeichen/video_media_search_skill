## Caption

Painful period cramps may be common, but in traditional Chinese medicine, they are not considered normal.

From a TCM perspective, cramps are often caused by cold and poor circulation in the uterus.

Simple habits like avoiding cold foods and drinks, wearing socks to keep the meridians warm, and protecting your lower back can help improve blood flow and reduce pain naturally.

If you experience cramps every month, your body may be asking for warmth and better circulation—not stronger painkillers.
