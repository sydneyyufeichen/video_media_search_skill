What are Chinese women actually drinking during perimenopause and menopause? Hint, it's not green juice or ice lattes. In traditional Chinese medicine, cold drinks are thought to weaken digestion and drain your energy. That's why women keep their bodies warm by sipping hot water all day. But not just plain hot water. They'll add slices of ginger, a few goji berries, and red dates into their thermals before heading to work. Here's why. Ginger warms the body and supports circulation. Goji berries are said to nourish the eyes, liver, and vitality. And red dates are famous in TCM for strengthening blood and calming the mind. Pro tip, cut the red date in half so it infuses better. Then pour in boiling water, seal it, and let it sit for an hour. What you get is a naturally-brewed tonic that women sip throughout the day. Instead of coffee or cold drinks, this warm infusion helps them balance hormones, ease fatigue, and feel steady through menopause. So from a TCM perspective, this isn't just a drink. It's daily self-care in a cup. Try it for yourself and see how your body feels.
