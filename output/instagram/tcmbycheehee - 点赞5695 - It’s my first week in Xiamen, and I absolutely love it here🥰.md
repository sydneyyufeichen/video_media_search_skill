## Caption

It’s my first week in Xiamen, and I absolutely love it here🥰

Our campus is ranked one of the most beautiful campuses in China, not to mention that the food is cheap and delicious. As a vegetarian, I thought I would struggle to find food in China, but turns out there’s plenty of options for me🥹 I feel so blessed and at home here, and I can’t wait to share more about my time here with you guys🥰

Our campus has a nature theme intermixed with Chinese cultural elements. There’s a beach right beside our university gate, so we can come out and enjoy the sunset every evening. There are also black swans by the university lake that are huge and majestic. Did you know that immersing yourself in nature is a way to relieve study stress? I feel so at peace and calm here 😌
