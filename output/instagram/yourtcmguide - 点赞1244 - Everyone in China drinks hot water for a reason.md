## Script

Everyone in China drinks hot water, and it's not just habit, it's how we protect our digestion. In traditional Chinese medicine, we believe the body runs on warmth. Our internal temperature is around 37 degrees Celsius. That's what our organs need to function properly. Now imagine pouring ice water into that system. In TCM, your stomach is like a cooking pot. If you keep adding cold, the fire weakens and digestion slows down. That's why even room temperature water is considered cooling in Chinese medicine. Over time, too much cold, like ice drinks, smoothies, raw foods, can weaken digestion. And that's when you start seeing things like bloating, low energy, and poor absorption. Instead, we lean toward warm. Warm water throughout the day, warm cooked meals, and avoid ice cold drinks, especially with your food. That's why in China, hot water isn't a trend, it's daily maintenance. And once you feel the difference, it's hard to go back. Follow to learn how simple habits like this shape your health.
