## Script

If you feel tired at 2 p.m., don't fight it. Take a nap, but do it the right way. In traditional Chinese medicine, your body follows the organ clock. Between 1 and 3 p.m., your small intestine meridian is at its peak. This is when your body is separating the pure from the impure, absorbing nutrients and redistributing energy throughout your system. That afternoon crash, it's not laziness. It's your qi shifting inward. If you take a short nap during this window, no longer than one hour, you actually support this natural reset. But here's the important part. Wake up before 3 p.m. because after 3 p.m., the bladder meridian becomes active. Your body starts mobilizing energy outward again. If you're still asleep during this phase, you can throw off your rhythm, and that may affect your nighttime sleep. And remember this, you need energy to sleep. That's why when you're sick, you feel exhausted but can't sleep deeply. Your body is using all its qi to fight, leaving not enough to anchor restful sleep. A strategic nap restores just enough qi so your body can fully relax at night. Try this for one week. Between 1 and 3 p.m., take a short nap and tell me how your nights change.
