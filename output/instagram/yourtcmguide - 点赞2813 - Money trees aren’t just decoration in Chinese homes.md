## Caption

Money trees aren’t just decoration in Chinese homes.
From a Chinese medicine and feng shui perspective, they represent living Qi—active, growing energy that influences how a space feels and how your body responds to it.

In Chinese medicine, your environment affects your nervous system, digestion, focus, and emotional balance. When the Qi in your home is strong and alive, you think more clearly, feel more regulated, and make better decisions—especially around work, money, and daily life.

That’s why the money tree is associated with wealth. Not because it magically gives you money, but because it supports the conditions that allow abundance and stability to grow over time.

## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say the money tree doesn't just attract wealth, but supports better energy in your home? In Chinese culture, the money tree is often linked to money and abundance, but in traditional Chinese medicine and in feng shui, it goes deeper than that. Plants represent living qi, active growing energy, and in feng shui, money is also considered a form of qi. So when you place a healthy thriving plant in your home, you're strengthening the energy flow of that space. The money tree grows upward with four rounded leaves. That upward growth symbolizes expansion, circulation, and steady progress. Not quick luck, but long-term stability. From a traditional Chinese medicine perspective, your environment directly affects your body. A space with strong, alive qi supports better digestion, clearer thinking, calmer nerves, and more grounded emotions. And when your body and mind are regulated, you naturally make better decisions around work, money, and life. So it's not that the money tree magically gives you money. It supports the conditions that allow wealth to grow.
