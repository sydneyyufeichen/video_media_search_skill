## Caption

I don’t have it all figured out. And that’s okay.

1. After studying Traditional Chinese Medicine for five years, I became an official acupuncturist and Chinese medicine practitioner. But even with that achievement, I realized—maybe this isn’t all I want. And that’s okay too.

2. I don’t have the luxury of pausing to find the “right” answer. Instead, I keep moving, trying, and learning. Because done is better than perfect—and perfect is never done.

3. A dream job? A dream life? You don’t have to wait for them to appear. You get to choose happiness. You get to build the life you want. And that’s the mindset I chose to live by.

4. I’ve stopped caring about what others think, though social media sometimes tries to convince me otherwise. I use it to reach the people who truly vibe with me. I believe that somewhere out there, my people do exist. No matter how far they are, this beautiful technology will bring the right ones to my accounts.

5. I want to be healthy, and teach others how to live a healthy life. In Chinese medicine, I learned that balance is the key to good health, and it’s the same for life. I’m creating my own routines, spending more time getting to know myself, and embracing every emotion as valid.

You can change your mind. You can explore different paths. You can take up space and be fully yourself.

I’m still figuring things out, and honestly? Life is quite fun. I don’t really know if what I write makes any sense, but who says it needs to make sense? If you’re on the same journey, let’s do this together.
