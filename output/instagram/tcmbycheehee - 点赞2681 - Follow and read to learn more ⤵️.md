1. Moxibustion:
Moxibustion is a traditional Chinese therapy that involves burning dried mugwort (Artemisia vulgaris) near specific acupuncture points on the body. This therapy aims to stimulate and regulate the flow of Qi (vital energy) and blood, promoting overall well-being. It is believed to be effective in relieving pain, improving circulation, and strengthening the immune system. 🌿🔥

2. Acupuncture:
Acupuncture is an ancient practice that uses thin needles inserted into precise acupuncture points to balance the body’s energy and restore health. It is known to help with various conditions such as pain management, stress reduction, and improving the body’s natural healing abilities.

3. Tuina Massage:
Tuina is a therapeutic Chinese massage technique that involves manipulating soft tissues and acupressure points to alleviate tension and promote relaxation. This treatment can improve circulation, relieve muscle pain, and address specific health concerns by restoring the body’s energy balance. 💆‍♂️💆‍♀️

4. Chinese Medicinal Herbs:
Chinese medicinal herbs are an essential component of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). These herbs are carefully selected and combined to create customized herbal formulas that address specific health issues. They can be used to enhance vitality, boost immunity, and support various aspects of well-being. 🌿💊
