## Script

Stop taking vitamins. If your body can't absorb them, they can build up as dampness and make you feel worse. In Chinese medicine, we don't just look at what you take. We look at whether your body can use it. Because every vitamin you take has to be processed by your digestion, what we call your spleen system. If that system is weak, those vitamins don't get fully absorbed. So instead of nourishing you, your body doesn't really use them. It either eliminates them or struggles to process them properly. And in TCM, when transformation is weak, that can lead to dampness. Over time, that can show up as fatigue, bloating, brain fog, heaviness, stubborn weight gain, hormonal imbalance. That's why some people take vitamins every day and still don't feel better. And if you think about it, you're adding more and more in without fixing the system that's supposed to handle it. So instead of relying on vitamins, we focus on something simpler. Get nutrients from real food. Warm cooked meals, simple ingredients your body recognizes, and habits that actually support digestion. Because in Chinese medicine, health doesn't come from more input. It comes from better transformation. When your body can actually process nutrients, you don't need extra vitamins.
