Can’t Sleep? 🌙🌿[read]

In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), restful sleep is closely linked to the balance of the Heart, Liver, and Spleen. These natural ingredients can calm the mind, nourish the body, and promote relaxation, making them excellent choices for improving sleep quality.

1. Lavender (薰衣草)

🌿 Benefits: Soothes the mind and reduces stress, promoting a sense of calm. Helps regulate emotions and alleviate mild insomnia.

✨ How to Use: Brew dried lavender flowers into a calming herbal tea before bedtime. Add a small handful of lavender to a sachet and place it near your pillow for a gentle aromatherapy effect.

2. Lily Bulb (百合)

🌿 Benefits: Nourishes the Heart and calms the mind, helping to ease restlessness and anxiety.

✨ How to Use: Add dried lily bulbs to soups, congee, or herbal teas. Combine with lotus seeds or jujubes for an enhanced calming effect.

3. Lotus Seed (莲子)

🌿 Benefits: Tonifies the Spleen and Heart, helping to relieve palpitations and irritability. Supports deeper, uninterrupted sleep by calming the mind.

✨ How to Use: Cook lotus seeds in sweet soups or desserts.

4. Longan (龙眼)

🌿 Benefits: Nourishes the Heart and Spleen, reducing fatigue and promoting relaxation. Known for its mild sedative properties, helping to improve sleep quality.

✨ How to Use: Add dried longan to herbal teas or desserts.

5. Jujube (红枣)

🌿 Benefits: Calms the mind and nourishes Blood, which are essential for restful sleep. Eases anxiety and promotes a feeling of tranquility.

✨ How to Use: Boil red dates with longan and lily bulbs for a warm and soothing tea. Add to soups or desserts to enhance their nourishing properties.

Bonus Tip: Create a relaxing bedtime ritual by pairing this tea with deep breathing exercises or a light foot massage to help you unwind fully. Sweet dreams! 🌙✨
