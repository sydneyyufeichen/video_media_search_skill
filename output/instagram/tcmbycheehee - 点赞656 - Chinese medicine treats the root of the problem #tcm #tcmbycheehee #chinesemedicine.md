## Script

Some people say that Chinese medicine is about curing the root of the problem. I disagree with that point. What I see is that Chinese medicine does both. We say that 急则治其表,缓则治其本. If it's emergency, for example, someone has a high fever, we want the temperature to go down. Then we do the quick fix. We use herbs that can quickly fix it. After we fix the problem, it doesn't have to be so rush. We treat the root of the problem. So either way, we still look at the pattern. What is the imbalance here? Is it yin deficiency, yang deficiency? Is it external? Is it internal? Once you identify the pattern, you can use the herbs that can counter it. And once you counter it, that helps it, be it the quick fix or the root balance.
