## Script

To all my Chinese baddies, if your digestion is off, nothing else in your body will feel right. In traditional Chinese medicine, digestion is the root of your energy. When it's weak, it shows up as bloating, low energy, brain fog, irregular stools, that heavy sluggish feeling. In TCM, we call that dampness. So if this sounds like you, here are three ways to fix it. First, chew your food and eat at consistent times. Digestion starts in your mouth, not your stomach. If you're rushing, scrolling, barely chewing, you're overloading your system. Slow down, chew more, eat on a regular schedule. Second, cut back on raw and cold foods. Sushi, cold smoothies, raw salads, they weaken your digestive fire. Your body has to spend extra energy just to warm them up. Warm cooked foods are much easier to process. Third, drink hot water throughout the day. Not iced, not cold, warm to hot. In TCM, your stomach is like a cooking pot. Heat keeps everything moving. Cold slows it down. Fix your digestion, and you fix your energy, your skin, even your mood. Which one are you fixing today? Comment below.
