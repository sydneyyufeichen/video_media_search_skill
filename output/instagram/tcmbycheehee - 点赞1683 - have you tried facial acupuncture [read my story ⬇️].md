have you tried facial acupuncture? [read my story ⬇️]

When I first started learning acupuncture, I practiced on myself to see how it felt. I did many tests on parts of my body to see if it really works.

Initially I didn’t see any results from facial acupuncture. So one day I decided to try something wild. I only placed needles on one side of my face.

The results were so obvious. The half that was treated was lifted, while the other side of my face didn’t change at all.

When I went to class, my friends asked me what happened to my face. So I checked my reflection and realized it was too obvious. The unbalance was REAL! So I went back and did the other side right after class. And I was fully lifted😆
