## Script

Eating healthy in Chinese medicine has nothing to do with calories. In Chinese medicine, food isn't just protein, vitamins, and carbs. We also care about the energy of the food, how it makes your body feel. For example, foods like ginger, garlic, and cinnamon are warming. They tend to make your body feel more active and warm. On the other side, foods like watermelon, cucumber, and lettuce are cooling and help your body release heat. Then there are neutral foods, the foundation of the diet. Rice, oats, eggs, carrots, broccoli, potatoes, and chicken, just to name a few. These foods support your body day to day without pushing your body too hot or too cold. So the goal isn't to eat perfectly. It's to balance warming and cooling foods, using neutral foods as your base, and avoid extremes like ice cold drinks, too much raw food, or living on coffee and sugar for energy. When you eat this way, your body gives you clearer signals. Less bloating, more stable energy, better focus. Food is medicine. When you eat for balance, you feel the difference.
