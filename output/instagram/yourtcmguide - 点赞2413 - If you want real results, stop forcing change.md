## Script

If you want real change in your life or your health, stop forcing it. The best strategy is to make it so subtle your body and everyone else doesn't resist it. This comes from an ancient Chinese playbook called the 36 Stratagems, a system of strategies used for centuries in war, business, and human behavior. And this is the very first one. It's called 满天过海, hide the truth to cross the sea. Meaning, the best way to create change is to make it almost unnoticeable. In life, this means building quietly, not announcing every plan, not forcing results too early, just building without drawing attention. And in health, it's the same thing. Your body resists extremes, crash diets, intense workouts, sudden changes. But small consistent adjustments, your body accepts those and it actually adapts. That's why the most powerful transformations don't feel dramatic. They happen under the surface, day by day, until the result is already there.
