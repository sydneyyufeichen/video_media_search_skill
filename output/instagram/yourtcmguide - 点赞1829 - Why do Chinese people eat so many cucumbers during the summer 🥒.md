## Script

I'm going to tell you why everyone in China eats more cucumbers in the summer, and so should you. No, it's not because they're low-calorie, and it's not because they're trendy. Chinese people have been eating cucumbers during hot weather for centuries. But why? Ever notice how in the summer you get thirstier, more irritable, break out more, and feel completely exhausted by the afternoon? In Chinese medicine, cucumbers are considered one of the best foods for hot weather. They're believed to help cool the body, replenish fluids, and relieve some of that internal heat that builds up during the summer. That's one reason they're so popular throughout China once temperatures start rising. Because cucumbers are mostly water, they're naturally refreshing on a hot day. Honestly, my favorite way to eat them is the simplest way possible, raw. Just wash a cucumber and eat it like a snack. It's refreshing, hydrating, and perfect for a hot summer day. But if you want something with a little more flavor, here's a classic Chinese preparation. Slice one cucumber into bite-sized pieces. Add a little minced garlic, a splash of vinegar, and a pinch of salt. Mix it all together and let it sit for about five minutes. That's it. So if you've been feeling hot, thirsty, and drained lately, try adding more cucumbers to your diet. Your body just might thank you for it.
