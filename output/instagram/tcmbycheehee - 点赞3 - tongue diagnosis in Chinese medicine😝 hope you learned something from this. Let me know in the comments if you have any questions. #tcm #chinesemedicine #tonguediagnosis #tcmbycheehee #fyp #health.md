So what does a Chinese medicine practitioner actually see when they look at your tongue? They look at a few things. They look at the color of your tongue, the size of your tongue, and the coating of your tongue. If you have a pale tongue, it's probably related to yang deficiency or qi deficiency, which is a lack of energy. So this person might feel really tired all the time. So on the other hand, if the tongue is too red, it means that there's too much heat and this person might be under a lot of stress, especially if the tip of their tongue is really red. If the tongue color is purple, it means that this person might have what we call blood stagnation. Or this can also be interpreted as poor circulation or having bad blood in the body. Besides the tongue color, we also look at the size of the tongue. So if the size of the tongue is too big, there will be teeth marks imprinted on the size of the tongue, causing what we call the teeth marks or scalloping. And this is what we call qi deficiency. And this is usually related to a weak digestion. Having a skinny tongue, on the other hand, means that this person might have a lot of heat, they might be skinnier, and they might not have enough yin substance or what we call blood in the body. And number three, when we look at the coating of the tongue, if there's almost no coating, it means that there's yin deficiency. So this person might feel thirsty all the time. They might have night sweats. They might be really skinny because they don't have enough yin in the body. A normal person's tongue has a thin white coating. On the other hand, if you have a very thick coating, this is related to having too much phlegm and dampness in the body.
