## Script

Greek yogurt is one of those things that traditional Chinese medicine has very mixed feelings about. Because yes, it does have protein. But from a TCM perspective, it can also quietly wreck your digestion. See, in Chinese medicine, Greek yogurt is considered cold and damp forming. And cold doesn't just mean temperature. It means it slows your body's digestive system, almost like turning the heat down on a stove while you're still cooking. Over time, that can weaken your stomach and spleen energy. That's when people start feeling bloated after eating, heavier or sluggish, tired in the afternoon, or like food just sits in their stomach. And because it's dairy, TCM says it can create phlegm and dampness. So if you constantly deal with sinus congestion, mucus, puffiness, acne, or that sticky feeling in your throat, TCM says Greek yogurt might not be helping you as much as you think. But if you love Greek yogurt, here's a TCM way to make it easier on your body. Don't eat it ice cold straight from the fridge. Let it warm up a little first. And pair it with warming foods like cinnamon, walnuts, dates, cooked fruit, or goji berries. Because in TCM, balance matters more than whether a food is simply labeled healthy.
