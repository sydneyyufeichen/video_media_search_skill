## Script

Cardio after 5pm, that might be the worst time for your body. The best time, before 8am. Here's why. In Chinese medicine, your body runs on an internal clock. Between 5am and 7am, your system is clearing out waste and ready to move. By 7 to 9am, your stomach is gearing up for food, not heavy exercise. And that's why the ideal time for cardio is before 8am, and at the latest, 9. At 5pm, your organs are shifting to recovery mode. Cardio then raises stress hormones, makes it harder to rest at night, and slows down recovery. So even if you push yourself, you don't get the same benefit. If you want more energy during the day, and better sleep at night, try finishing your cardio before 8am. Try it for a week and let me know how it feels.
