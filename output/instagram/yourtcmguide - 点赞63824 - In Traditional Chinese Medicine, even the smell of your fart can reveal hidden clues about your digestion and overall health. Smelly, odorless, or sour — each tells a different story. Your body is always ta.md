## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say the smell of your fart is like a health report card? Because that smell can reveal exactly what's going on deep inside your digestion. It's your body sending you a signal. If your fart is extra smelly, that often points to damp heat in your gut. Think greasy food, fried meals, sweets, or alcohol. All of that mixes together and creates rotting heavy energy in the stomach and intestines. If your fart is odorless but constant, that's usually weak digestion. Your body doesn't have enough fire to fully transform food into energy, so it just keeps producing gas again and again, leaving you bloated or tired. And if the smell is sour or rotten, that's a sign of food stagnation. Meals sitting in your stomach too long, fermenting and producing toxins that can affect your skin or mood over time. In traditional Chinese medicine, your stomach is like a cooking pot. If the flame is weak, the food stays raw. If the pot is too hot and damp, the food ferments and burns. The smell of your fart is like the smoke escaping from that pot, a clue about whether your digestive fire is balanced or not. So the smell of your fart isn't random, it's your body sending you a signal about what's going on inside. And in Chinese medicine, paying attention to those small signals is the first step to better health.
