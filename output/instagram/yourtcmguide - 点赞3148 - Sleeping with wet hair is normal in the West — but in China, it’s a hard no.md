## Script

News flash. Women in China don't go to bed with wet hair. In fact, if you try this at your mom's house in China, she'd chase you down with a towel and a hair dryer. In Western culture, going to bed with wet hair is pretty normal. If there's any warning at all, it's usually about hair damage, like frizz or breakage. But in Chinese medicine, wet hair isn't just a hair issue, it's a health issue. When your hair is wet, that moisture and cool air can let what we call wind and cold enter the body. And when you're asleep, your body is relaxed and repairing, which makes it more vulnerable. In Chinese medicine, the head and neck are where many important meridians pass through. Cold exposure here is believed to affect circulation to the brain and sinuses, which is why people link this habit to headaches and neck stiffness. That's also why people in China avoid sleeping directly under a fan or AC, and why they'll cover their neck even in warm weather. So if you shower at night, just make sure your hair is fully dry before bed. Try it for one week and let me know how you feel.
