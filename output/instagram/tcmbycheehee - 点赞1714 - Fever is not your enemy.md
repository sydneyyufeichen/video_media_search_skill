## Caption

Fever is not your enemy. 

It’s one of your body’s natural defense mechanisms: a controlled rise in temperature that makes it harder for viruses and bacteria to thrive, while supporting your immune system.

You don’t always need to rush to ice packs and cold drinks the moment a fever appears. 

For mild fever in an otherwise stable adult:
1 Rest
2 Drink plenty of warm fluids
3 Wear light, breathable clothing
4 From a Chinese medicine perspective, certain gentle herbs like ginger and cinnamon are used to promote a light sweat and help the body release heat.

🚨But if the fever is very high, lasts several days, affects babies or the elderly, or comes with symptoms like difficulty breathing, chest pain, stiff neck, confusion, seizures, or persistent vomiting, please seek medical care immediately.

Support your body first. Cool down wisely, not blindly. 🫶
