## Script

Everyone thinks the gallbladder isn't essential. You can take it out and still live, right? But in traditional Chinese medicine, your gallbladder does so much more than just digest fat. It's actually your organ of courage and decision-making, literally the part of your body that helps you take action in life. In TCM, your gallbladder and liver are like best friends. Your liver plans and your gallbladder executes. Together, they control the smooth flow of tea, your body's life energy. When your gallbladder tea is strong, you feel brave, decisive, confident. You trust your gut. But when it's weak or blocked, you hesitate, second-guess yourself, or feel easily overwhelmed. Ever felt paralyzed by choices, like even small decisions stress you out? That's what we call liver-gallbladder tea stagnation. And it often shows up physically too. Bloating, nausea, headaches, bitter taste in mouth, or tight shoulders and neck. So if you want a strong gallbladder and strong decisions, avoid greasy, heavy foods, sleep before 11 p.m., and eat more sour foods like lemon and vinegar to help bile flow. Because in TCM, your gallbladder doesn't just digest fat. It digests life itself.
