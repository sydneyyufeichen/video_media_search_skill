## Script

Do you know why almost everyone in China takes a nap after lunch? It's not just habit, it's medicine. According to traditional Chinese medicine, your body follows something called the organ clock. It's the idea that your body's energy flows through different organ systems at different times of the day. From 1 to 3 p.m., it's small intestine time. This is when your body is finishing the work of digesting lunch and transforming nutrients into usable energy. Instead of reaching for another cup of coffee, Chinese medicine recommends taking a short nap. And during the summer, this habit becomes even more important. According to traditional Chinese medicine, the heat of summer gradually uses up your tea and body fluids. A short afternoon nap gives your body a chance to recover and recharge before the rest of your day. You don't need to sleep for an hour. Even 10 to 20 minutes is enough. So if you feel sleepy after lunch this summer, don't fight it. Your body may simply be following its natural rhythm.
