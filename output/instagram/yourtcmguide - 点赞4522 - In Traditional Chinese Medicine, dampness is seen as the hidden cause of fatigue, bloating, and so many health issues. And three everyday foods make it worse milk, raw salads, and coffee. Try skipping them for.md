Knock knock, this is your TCM guide. If you want to change your health, it's not about more gym time or more supplements. The real shift starts with what you stop eating. In traditional Chinese medicine, one hidden cause behind many diseases is dampness. Dampness makes you feel heavy, sluggish, and stuck. Over time, it blocks circulation, weakens digestion, and sets the stage for chronic issues. And three everyday foods feed dampness the most. Milk, raw salads, and coffee. Milk, it's heavy and damp. Perfect for a baby cow, but in us, it clogs digestion, weakens the body, and turns into phlegm. Raw salads, they seem light and healthy, but they're cold and hard to break down. That slows your system, creates dampness, and leaves you bloated, tired, and low on energy. Coffee, it feels like quick energy, but long term, it drains your reserves. And when digestion is weakened, dampness follows, even if you think that morning cup is helping. So here's the challenge, cut out milk, raw salads, and coffee for one month. Keep moving your body, but notice how much lighter, clearer, and more energized you feel when dampness isn't holding you back.
