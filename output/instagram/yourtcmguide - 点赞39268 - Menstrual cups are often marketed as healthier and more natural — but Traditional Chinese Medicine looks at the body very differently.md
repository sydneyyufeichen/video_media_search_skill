## Script

Newsflash. Women in China do not use menstrual cups. In fact, if you're trying to find menstrual cups in Chinese supermarkets or in Chinese drugstores, you'll struggle to find one. And there's actually a traditional Chinese medicine reason why. In TCM, your period is meant to flow down and out freely. Menstrual blood is not something you're supposed to hold inside the body. A menstrual cup does exactly that. It collects blood inside the body for hours. From a TCM perspective, that creates blood stagnation. And stagnation is a root cause of cramps, clots, bloating, and hormonal imbalance. Another key principle in Chinese medicine is warmth. The uterus must stay warm during your period to keep circulation smooth. Anything inserted into the body, yes, even menstrual cup, is considered cold. Cold slows blood flow and slow flow equals pain. Menstrual cups are often marketed as healthier or more natural. But in TCM, health isn't just about materials. It's about movement. Even if it's medical-grade silicone, blocking the body's natural release is still a problem. So while menstrual cups are popular in the West, many women in China avoid them to protect circulation, warmth, and long-term reproductive health.
