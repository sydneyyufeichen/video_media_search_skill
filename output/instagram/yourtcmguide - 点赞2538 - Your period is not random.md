Do you know why in Chinese medicine, we say your menstrual cycle is a monthly health report card? When it becomes irregular, that's your body trying to tell you something's wrong. One of the luckiest things about being a woman is that your body gives you this report every month. When your period comes around the same time, that usually means your system is in balance. For most people, that's around a 28 to 31 day cycle, bleeding for about 5 to 7 days. Some people naturally have shorter or longer cycles, and that can still be normal for them. But if your cycle is all over the place, 25 days one month, 40 the next, or skipping months, that's not random. That's your body asking for attention. In Chinese medicine, a woman's health is rooted in the liver system. It regulates stress and emotions, stores blood, and keeps everything flowing on time. So chronic stress, poor sleep, lack of movement, cold plunges, or ice drinks disrupt that flow. And over time, irregular periods develop.
