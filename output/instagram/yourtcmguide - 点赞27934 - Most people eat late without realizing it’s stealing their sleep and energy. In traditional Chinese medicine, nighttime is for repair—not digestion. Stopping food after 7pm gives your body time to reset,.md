## Script

Do you know why in traditional Chinese medicine, we say you should stop eating after sunset? This sounds extreme today, but in ancient China, people usually only ate two meals a day. Breakfast in the morning between 7 and 9 a.m. and lunch in the early afternoon around 1 to 3 p.m. After that, nothing. Not because food was scarce, but because they believed the evening was meant for the body to slow down, repair, and recover. Eating late keeps your body working when it should be resting. In Chinese medicine, we follow something called the organ clock. Every two hours, a different organ is most active. At night, your body is no longer focused on digestion. It's focused on detoxing, repairing tissues, and restoring energy. Modern research now echoes this idea. Stopping food earlier gives your body time to clean up old, weak cells, a process linked to better sleep, metabolism, and longevity. So try this for seven days. No food or drinks except water after 7 p.m. Most people notice deeper sleep, less bloating, and more energy in the morning.
