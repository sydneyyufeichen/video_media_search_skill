## Script

Everyone says trust the experts, but in traditional Chinese medicine, we say the first person you should trust is yourself. Why? Because every body is different. Your history, your habits, your energy, they're not the same as anyone else's. That's why Chinese medicine never uses a one-size-fits-all formula. It looks at the whole body, not just one small piece of advice. But here's the catch. Science and experts often look for one universal solution. That might look good in a study, but in real life, it doesn't always fit. Two people can follow the same advice and end up with totally different results. That's where intuition comes in. Your body is giving you signals all the time. When you listen, you'll know what supports you and what doesn't. And the more you pay attention, the clearer those signals become. Almost like your body is training you to understand it better. So don't hand all your power away. Trust your intuition, use your common sense, and remember, nobody knows your body better than you.
