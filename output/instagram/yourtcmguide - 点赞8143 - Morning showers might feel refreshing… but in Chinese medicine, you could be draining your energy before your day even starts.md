## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine, we say you should never shower first thing in the morning? In traditional Chinese medicine, your body runs on yang qi, which is your internal warmth, energy, and metabolism. In the morning, that energy is just starting to rise, like the sun coming up. Your body is trying to wake up your circulation, digestion, and brain. But if you jump straight into the shower, especially a cold one, you're exposing your body to water when it's trying to build heat. In TCM, water suppresses fire. So instead of supporting your energy, you're dampening it right at the start of the day. That's why some people wake up feeling tired, foggy, and low energy. Instead, mornings should be about building energy first. Move your body, drink something warm, let your system fully wake up. And if you want to know the best time to shower, do it at night. A warm shower before bed helps your body relax, improve circulation, and sets you up for deeper sleep. Just make sure to fully dry your hair before sleeping.
