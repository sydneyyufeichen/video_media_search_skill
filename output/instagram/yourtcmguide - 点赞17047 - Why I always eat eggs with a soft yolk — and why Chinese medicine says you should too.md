## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say you should always eat your eggs with a soft, runny yolk? Because the way you cook your egg actually changes its energy, and that changes what it does inside your body. From a traditional Chinese medicine perspective, eggs are deeply nourishing. The yolk especially is rich in blood-building and yin-nourishing properties. But here's the key. If you fully hard-cook the yolk, you change its nature. A soft yolk keeps the egg moist, nourishing, and easier to digest. In TCM, we say foods that preserve their natural moisture help support yin, which is crucial if you run dry, hot, stressed, or exhausted. A fully cooked chalky yolk that becomes drier and more heating. If you're already dealing with dryness, irritability, insomnia, constipation, or hormone imbalance, a hard-cooked yolk can actually make those symptoms worse. That's why in Chinese medicine, congee with a soft egg, ramen with a soft egg, steamed eggs, these are all considered strengthening for the spleen, liver, and kidneys. So if you want better digestion, steadier energy, and more nourishment from your food, here's the simplest tip. Eat your eggs with a soft runny yolk. It protects the yin, nourishes the blood, and is so much easier on your body.
