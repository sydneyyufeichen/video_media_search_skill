## Script

While many people are focused on Easter today, your body just entered a completely new season. In Chinese medicine, this happens around this time every year. It's called Qingming, one of the 24 solar terms. These solar terms were used for thousands of years to track how nature changes, and how your body changes with it. Qingming means clear and bright. The weather gets noticeably warmer, rainfall increases, and plants start growing rapidly. This is when trees start leafing out, grass turns greener, flowers begin to bloom, and everything in nature is moving upward and outward. It's a season of growth, movement, and expansion. But your body is doing the same thing. In Chinese medicine, spring is connected to your liver, and during Qingming, your liver energy is rising quickly. That's why people often feel more stressed, more irritable, more tension in the body, or even headaches and digestion issues. Your body is trying to expand and move, just like nature. But if that energy gets stuck, that's when problems show up. So even small things matter right now. Go outside and move your body, eat lighter, fresher foods, and don't hold in your stress or emotions. I'll break down exactly what to do and avoid next, but for now, just remember, follow the season and your body works with you.
