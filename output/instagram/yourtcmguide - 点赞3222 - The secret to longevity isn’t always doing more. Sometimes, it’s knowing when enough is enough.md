## Script

If you want to live a long life, Chinese medicine says there's one thing you should avoid. Excess. Too much food, alcohol, exercise, work, even too much emotion. Because in traditional Chinese medicine, more is not always better. The goal is balance. Think about nature. Too much sun creates drought. Too much rain creates floods. Too much heat burns. And too much cold freezes. Your body is the same. Eat too much and you burden your digestion. Exercise too much and instead of building energy, you deplete it. Work too much without resting and eventually your body has nothing left to give. Even emotions can become harmful when they become excessive. Too much anger, worry, fear. Chinese medicine teaches us that health comes from living in harmony with nature. And nature always moves toward balance. That's why one of the secrets to longevity isn't adding more things to your life. Sometimes it's knowing when enough is enough. Eat, but don't overeat. Exercise, but don't exhaust yourself. Work, but also rest. Enjoy life, but don't live in excess. Because the goal isn't to push your body as hard as possible. The goal is to preserve your body as long as possible.
