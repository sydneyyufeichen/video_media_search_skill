📍 5-Min Gua Sha Routine:

1️⃣ Press acupoints gently for activation

2️⃣ Glide from nose to cheekbones

3️⃣ Lift under-eye & apple cheeks

4️⃣ Follow jawline to ears

5️⃣ Sweep upward from chin to temples

6️⃣ Drain down behind ears & neck

💡 Repeat each step 10x with light pressure

⚠️ Avoid These Mistakes:

❌ Never dry scrape – always use face oil (Joi Oil recommended!)

❌ Don’t drag downward – always lift upward

❌ Too much pressure? – keep it gentle to avoid bruising
