Hey guys, I'm going to tell you why everyone in China starts drinking soy milk in the summer, and so should you. And no, it's not some new wellness trend. Chinese people have been doing this for hundreds of years. The moment summer arrives, soy milk becomes a breakfast staple in many parts of China. But why? According to traditional Chinese medicine, one of the biggest challenges of summer is that the heat gradually uses up your body's fluids. Over time, that can leave you feeling thirsty, tired, overheated, sleeping poorly, or dealing with dry skin or a dry mouth. That's where soy milk comes in. In Chinese medicine, soybeans are known for helping nourish body fluids while also helping clear heat. That's why so many people enjoy soy milk throughout the summer. But here's the part that surprises most people. They often drink it warm or at room temperature, not ice cold. Because in traditional Chinese medicine, constantly drinking icy beverages is thought to make it harder for your digestive system to function at its best. So if the summer heat has you feeling drained, try replacing one cold drink with a glass of warm or room temperature fresh soy milk. It might be one of the simplest Chinese medicine summer habits worth trying.
