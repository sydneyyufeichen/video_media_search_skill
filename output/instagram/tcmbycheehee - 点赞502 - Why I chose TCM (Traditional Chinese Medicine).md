## Script

What is my motivation for choosing TCM? Story time. Eight years ago, I was studying in a Buddhist high school in Northern California. It's called the City of 10,000 Buddhas. It's located inside a temple. So back then, there were TCM practitioners who would go there and volunteer. I went to the clinic and I was like, I want to have a body checkup. And there was a TCM practitioner who took my pulse. After just taking my pulse and looking at my tongue, immediately she knew so much about me that I didn't even tell her yet. And that got me interested. She prescribed some herbs for me. And then she told me to lay down and she started doing acupuncture on me. And the next thing I knew, I woke up and it's been a whole hour. I was in super, super deep sleep. I haven't felt so asleep for a very long time. And after the acupuncture, I got my herbs. And that was my first time cooking herbs for myself. So I took those herbs for a week. I felt like my digestion was restored. My energy levels were restored. I didn't know how to describe the feeling other than I feel like really great. It was at the end of my senior year when my teacher asked me, you're considering to go back home for your studies, right? You should consider Chinese medicine. I was like, huh, Chinese medicine. Yeah, I remember it being very effective. The acupuncture and the herbs, that was great. And to me back then, it was really cool, especially the pulse taking part. After considering it, I told my teacher, I'm like, yeah, you're right. I think I want to study this. And then he gave me a whole book. It's the Huang Di Nei Jing, which is the Yellow Emperor's classic. And I was like, whoa, this is so cool. I gave that a read. And I was like, yep, this is my path. And I am excited to learn more and become a practitioner. I can't wait to do acupuncture for myself, for my family, for my friends. That's my motivation for studying Chinese medicine. That's why I am a practitioner now.
