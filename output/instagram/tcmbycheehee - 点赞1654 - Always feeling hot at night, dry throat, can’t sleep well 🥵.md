## Caption

Always feeling hot at night, dry throat, can’t sleep well? 🥵

It might not just be “stress”

in Chinese medicine, we call this Yin Deficiency (阴虚).

When Yin is low, the body loses its cooling, moistening, and calming power.
You may notice:
	•	Night sweats or heat in the palms, soles, chest
	•	Dry throat, dry eyes, dry skin
	•	Light, easily disturbed sleep
	•	Feeling “wired but tired”

One of the classic formulas for Yin Deficiency is: 六味地黄丸 (Liu Wei Di Huang Wan)

It’s traditionally used to nourish Kidney & Liver Yin, supporting:
	•	Lower back & knees
	•	Sleep and restfulness
	•	Essence and long-term vitality

But ⚠️ not everyone who feels “heat” should take 六味地黄丸.

In TCM, we always:
1️⃣ Look at your full body pattern (tongue, pulse, symptoms)
2️⃣ Differentiate if it’s Yin deficiency, Heat excess, Qi deficiency, Dampness, etc.
3️⃣ Adjust herbs and dosage based on your body.

So please don’t self-prescribe just because you saw this online 🙏
Instead, consult a qualified TCM practitioner to see if 六味地黄丸 is suitable for you.
