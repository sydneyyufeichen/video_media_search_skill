Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say you should never sleep with dolls or human figurines facing you? Let me explain. In feng shui and in traditional Chinese medicine, there's a concept called xiao ren, literally little people energy. Not actual people, but symbolic of gossip, tension, negative influences, the type of energy that drains you. And here's what most people don't realize. Anything shaped like a human, dolls, figurines, statues, even artwork with faces, can hold that type of energy when they're facing you. Especially at night. Because when you sleep, your body shifts from yang to yin, your guard drops, your qi becomes more open and more sensitive. So if something with a human form is watching you, even something that's decorative in your room, TCM says it can subtly disturb your qi, affect your dreams, and leave you waking up feeling off or more tired than you should. Feng shui also warns anything positioned above you, like figures on a shelf or porches overhead, can create a sense of pressure on your body and mind. It's not just physical, it's energetic. That's why in Chinese culture, we don't keep human-faced objects watching us sleep. Turn them away or move them out of your room, because if something is staring at you while you sleep, TCM says your energy never fully rests.
