## Script

Here are the top five things to avoid during your period, according to traditional Chinese medicine. Number one, cold drinks and cold foods. This is probably the most well-known one. According to Chinese medicine, cold is believed to slow the circulation of blood and qi, which is why many women avoid it, especially during the first few days of their period. Number two, staying up past 11 p.m. Your body needs extra rest while you're menstruating. In Chinese medicine, getting enough sleep is one of the best ways to support your body's recovery during your cycle. Number three, cold exposure. That means things like cold plunges, swimming in cold water, or sitting on cold surfaces for long periods. Traditionally, Chinese women are taught to keep their lower body warm during their period. Number four, skipping meals. Your body's already working hard, so this isn't the time for crash diets or fasting. Warm, nourishing meals are generally encouraged instead. And number five, pushing yourself through intense workouts. Light exercise is perfectly fine if you're up to it, but if you're exhausted, Chinese medicine recommends listening to your body instead of pushing through it. Whether you follow all of these or not, these are some of the most common period habits that have been passed down in Chinese medicine for generations.
