## Script

Hey guys, I'm going to tell you why everyone in China eats lamb during the winter, and it has nothing to do with taste. From a traditional Chinese medicine perspective, winter is the most yin season of the year. Cold weather slows circulation, weakens digestion, and drains your yang energy, your body's internal warmth. And lamb? Lamb is considered one of the warmest foods in Chinese medicine. In TCM, lamb strongly warms the kidneys and the spleen, which are the two systems most affected by cold weather. That's why in winter, especially in northern China, you'll see lamb soup, lamb hot pot, and slow-cooked lamb dishes everywhere. People aren't just eating it because it's comforting. It's functional food. You'll also notice lamb is rarely eaten cold or on its own. It's paired with hot broth, long cooking times, and warming spices like ginger and cumin, so the heat penetrates deeply instead of stressing digestion. In Chinese medicine, eating with the season isn't a trend. It's how you protect your energy and stay healthy year-round. So if you're always cold, tired, bloated, or bloated in winter, it might not be about eating more. It might be about eating warmer.
