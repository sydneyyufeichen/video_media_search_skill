## Script

Do you know why in traditional Chinese medicine we often say stop eating after sunset? In ancient China, people only ate two meals a day, breakfast in the morning between 7 and 9 a.m., and lunch in the early afternoon between 1 p.m. and 3 p.m. And after that, nothing. The idea is simple. Eating late makes it harder for your body to rest and recover. Modern research even shows something similar. When you stop eating earlier, your body uses that time to clean up old and weak cells. That helps you feel healthier and can even support longevity. So try this.
