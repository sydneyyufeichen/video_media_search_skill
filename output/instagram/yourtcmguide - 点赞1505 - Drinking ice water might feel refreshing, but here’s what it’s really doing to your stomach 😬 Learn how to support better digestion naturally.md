## Script

Do you drink ice water every day without giving it much thought? Well, traditional Chinese medicine, or TCM, says that simple habit could be working against your health. In traditional Chinese medicine, your stomach works best when it is warm, kind of like a pot on a stove. It needs that warmth to break down the food properly and help your body use the nutrients. But when you drink ice water, it cools everything down. It slows down digestion, affects circulation, and over time can lead to issues like bloating, low energy, and even hormonal imbalance. Drinking warm or hot water can help your body digest food more easily and keeps things running more smoothly.
