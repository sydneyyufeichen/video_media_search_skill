What is “Shang Huo” (Excess Heat)?🔥

In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), ”Shang Huo“ refers to an imbalance in the body where excess heat or fire manifests as symptoms like a sore throat, dry mouth, acne, or constipation. Two common types are Qi Stagnation Heat and Deficiency Heat in Weak Individuals.

1. Qi Stagnation Heat

Cause:  

- Qi Stagnation Heat occurs when the body‘s energy (Qi) flow is disrupted, causing heat to accumulate.  

- Over-supplementation (e.g., consuming excessive tonics like ginseng or blood-nourishing foods) can burden the body, especially if digestion and absorption are weak. This leads to ”internal heat“ building up and rising.  

- Emotional factors like stress or frustration can further block Qi flow, worsening the issue.  

Symptoms: Sore throat, Mouth ulcers, Constipation, Acne or skin irritation

2. Deficiency Heat in Weak Individuals

Cause:  
- Deficiency Heat is common in individuals with a weak constitution, especially those with Yin deficiency.  

- Yin deficiency means there’s insufficient ”cooling“ Yin fluids in the body to balance ”warming“ Yang energy, resulting in a sensation of heat rising (”Deficiency Fire“).  

- This is often seen in people who are weak but consume strong tonics (like deer antler or red ginseng) without proper guidance.  

Symptoms: Hot palms and soles, Night sweats, Dry throat, Dry mouth and red tongue tip  

Since different types of ”Shang Huo“ require different treatments, it’s best to consult a professional TCM practitioner for personalized advice if symptoms persist or worsen!
