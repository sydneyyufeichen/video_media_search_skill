## Script

Everyone tells you to drink more water, but in Chinese medicine, forcing fluids can actually harm your body. Here's why. In TCM, your body doesn't just absorb water. It first has to transform and transport it. That job belongs to the spleen and stomach, your digestive system. When you force yourself to drink water all day without feeling thirsty, you're pouring fluids into a system that may already be weak or overworked. Instead of hydrating you, it overwhelms digestion and creates dampness, which can show up as bloating, heaviness, fatigue, brain fog, puffiness, loose stools, or that heavy waterlogged feeling. Here's a key point most people miss. Thirst is a signal. It's your body telling you it's ready to receive and handle fluids. If you rarely feel thirsty, it's not a sign that you're well hydrated. It often means your fluid metabolism is already out of balance. And when you keep forcing fluids anyway, you're not helping. You're adding stress to a body that's already struggling behind the scenes. In Chinese medicine, hydration is about balance, not force. Drink when you're thirsty. Support digestion first and your body will regulate fluids naturally, without strain.
