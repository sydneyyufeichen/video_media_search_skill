This recipe is pure comfort, crafted to provide warmth and nourishment—ideal for mothers during their confinement period. Infused with the healing principles of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), aims to restore balance, replenish energy, and support your recovery journey.

Ingredients:

* 300g king trumpet mushroom
* 3 tbsp potato starch
* 6 pcs red dates
* 1 pc cinnamon stick
* 3 star anise
* 1 pc bay leaf
* 1/4 tsp salt
* ⁠1 tbsp oyster sauce
* ⁠1 tsp dark soy sauce
* ⁠10g rock sugar
* ⁠1 tsp cornstarch water

Instructions:

1. Gently pound the King Trumpet mushrooms until they break into smaller pieces.

2. Sprinkle salt over the mushrooms, squeeze out any excess water, then coat with potato starch until the mushrooms stick together.

3. Heat oil in a pan and deep fry the mushrooms until golden brown. Set them aside.

4. In the same pan, add oyster sauce, water, cinnamon stick, star anise, red dates and bay leaf. Stir to combine. Lastly add in the vegan meatball and let it simmer for 2mins.

5. Add a little cornstarch water to thicken the sauce, then serve with steamed white rice.

Enjoy this comforting, flavorful dish!
