If you have stiff or creaky joints, if you have tried glucosamine, collagen, and nothing really works, you're likely missing the key ingredient. Vitamin D. Yes, it's a total game changer. My mom had suffered from knee problems for almost 20 years, and she started feeling much better after taking vitamin D for only a week. You know, most of our vitamin D comes from sunlight hitting our skin, with only a small amount from foods like fatty fish. But many people don't get enough and are never aware of it. If you are one of the following groups, please pay attention. First, people who live in areas with less sunshine, of course. And second, seniors, because aging skin loses the ability to make it. Third, women going through menopause, as hormonal shifts can accelerate bone loss. And lastly, people with darker skin, because melanin acts like a natural shield. It protects you from UV rays, but it also blocks the sunlight needed to create vitamin D. So darker skin needs three or four times more sun exposure to gain the same benefits.
