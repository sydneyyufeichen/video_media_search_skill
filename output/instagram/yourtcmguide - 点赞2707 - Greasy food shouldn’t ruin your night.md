## Script

If you feel bloated, heavy, or sluggish after greasy food, this is the Chinese medicine drink you should know about. In traditional Chinese medicine, that heavy stuck feeling after a meal isn't random. It means your digestion is overloaded, especially by oily, fried, or rich foods. And one of the most common remedies we use is barley tea. Literally just barley, toasted and brewed into tea. In Chinese medicine, barley helps drain dampness and move food stagnation. Greasy foods create heaviness in the stomach and intestines, which is why you feel bloated, tired, or foggy. That's why in many Asian households, barley tea is served with or after heavy meals, hot pot barbecue fried foods, instead of cold drinks or soda. These tea bags make it super easy. Just drop it into hot water, let it steep for a few minutes, and drink it warm after eating. Warm liquids support digestion, cold drinks slow everything down. If greasy meals always leave you feeling uncomfortable, try this instead of antacids or cold drinks.!
