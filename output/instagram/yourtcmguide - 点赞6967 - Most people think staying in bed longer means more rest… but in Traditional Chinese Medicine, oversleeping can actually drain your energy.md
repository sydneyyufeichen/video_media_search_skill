## Caption

Most people think staying in bed longer means more rest… but in Traditional Chinese Medicine, oversleeping can actually drain your energy.

There’s a concept called 久卧耗气 — “lying too long consumes your Qi.”

When you stay in bed 9–10 hours, even awake, your circulation slows and your Qi becomes stagnant.
That’s why you wake up feeling heavy, groggy, or unrefreshed.

Aim for 7–8 hours and get out of bed within 10 minutes of waking to keep your Qi moving and your mornings energized.
