## Script

Hey guys, I've been reading Plato's Republic lately, and I came across this famous allegory of the cave, and it low-key got me thinking about traditional Chinese medicine. So in the story, there are these people chained inside a cave, and all they can see are shadows on a wall, and to them, those shadows are reality. They don't know there's a whole world outside the cave, and I was like, wait, this is how most people think about health these days. Because what we experience, headaches, bloating, fatigue, poor sleep, those are like the shadows. That's all we see, so we think that's the problem. But in TCM, we look at it differently. We're not trying to get rid of the shadow, we're trying to figure out what's creating it. Like, if you're always tired, it might not be a lack of energy problem. It could be your digestion is weak, your sleep timing is off, or your body just isn't functioning in sync. So instead of constantly reacting to symptoms, TCM is more about stepping back and asking yourself what's actually causing this. And honestly, once you see it that way, it's kind of hard to unsee. It's like stepping out of the cave for the first time.
