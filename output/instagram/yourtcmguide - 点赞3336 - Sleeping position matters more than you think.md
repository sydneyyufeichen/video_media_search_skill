Sleeping position matters more than you think.

In Traditional Chinese Medicine and Feng Shui, sleeping directly facing the door is believed to disturb your Qi while your body is in its most vulnerable, yin state.

That’s why this position has been warned against for centuries — not to scare you, but to protect deep rest and nervous system recovery.

Take a look at your bedroom tonight and notice how you wake up.

## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say you should never sleep facing the door? Let me explain. In feng shui and traditional Chinese medicine, this sleeping position actually has a name. It's often called the coffin position. Not to scare you, but to describe how energy moves through the body during sleep. At night, your body naturally shifts from yang to yin. Your protective qi naturally lowers. Your body softens, slows down, and becomes more receptive. But doors are where qi enters and exits the space. So when your bed directly faces the door, that moving energy is aimed straight at you all night long. TCM says this keeps your nervous system subtly alert. Your body never fully switches into deep rest mode. Even if you sleep for 7 or 8 hours, you may wake up tired, foggy, or tense. Some people also notice lighter sleep, sudden waking, or unusually vivid dreams. From a feng shui perspective, sleeping exposed to the door creates a sense of vulnerability. On a subconscious level, your body feels like it needs to stay watchful instead of relaxed. That's why Chinese medicine recommends placing your bed where you can see the door, but never align directly with it. If moving your bed isn't possible, even angling it slightly or adding a visual barrier can help.
