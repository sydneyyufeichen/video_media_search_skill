## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say your pee is like a health report card? Because the color, smell, and amount of urine can tell us exactly what's going on inside your body. If your urine is dark yellow and strong smelling, that usually means heat in the body. Think dehydration, too much alcohol, spicy food, or stress overheating your system. If it's very pale and you pee constantly, that usually points to weak kidney qi. Your body can't hold fluids properly, so everything just passes straight through, leaving you tired and cold. If your urine is cloudy, that's often dampness. Too many greasy foods, sugar, or dairy, creating heaviness your body can't fully clear. And if it burns or feels uncomfortable, that's damp heat in the lower body. Inflammation your system is trying to push out. In Chinese medicine, your kidneys are like the body's filtration system. When they're strong, fluids are clean and balanced. When they're weak or overheated, the signs show up immediately in your urine. So your pee isn't just pee, it's your body talking to you. And in Chinese medicine, listening early is how you prevent bigger problems later.
