## Script

I just saw the real-time proof of lowering sugar. My 70-year-old mom has been in prediabetes zone for about 4-5 years. She's been careful about her diet, but her fasting glucose still hands around 6.6. She was wearing a CGM today, and she wanted to check it out. So her glucose dropped from 7.8 to 6.8 in just 20 minutes. What happened during this 20 minutes? Believe it or not, no crazy workout, it was just a walk. So the secret is really simple. Eat healthy and move more. Even a short walk works. Your body is listening.
