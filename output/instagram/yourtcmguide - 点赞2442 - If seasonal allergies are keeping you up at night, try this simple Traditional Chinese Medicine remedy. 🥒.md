## Script

If seasonal allergies are keeping you up at night, I want to share one of the simplest remedies from traditional Chinese medicine. It's simple, soothing, and takes just a few cucumber slices. Sometimes the best remedies are the ones already sitting in your kitchen. Here's how it works. Cut a few very thin slices of cucumber, then place the slices on your nose and around your sinuses like a mini face mask. Leave them on for about 20 to 30 minutes, or if you want, you can even sleep with them on overnight. According to traditional Chinese medicine, seasonal allergies are often associated with heat accumulating in the upper part of the body. That's why your nose can feel stuffed up, why your sinuses can feel swollen, and why your face can feel hot and uncomfortable. Cucumber is considered one of the most cooling foods in Chinese medicine. It's traditionally used to help clear heat and calm irritation, which is why it has been used as a simple home remedy during allergy season for generations. Will cucumber cure your allergies? No. But if you're sick and tired of waking up with stuffy nose, congested sinuses, or that hot, swollen feeling, this simple bedtime ritual might be worth trying tonight.
