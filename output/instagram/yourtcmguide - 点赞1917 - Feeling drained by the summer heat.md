Feeling drained by the summer heat?
Try this traditional Chinese summer energy drink:
Add 1 cup mung beans, 1 gallon water, and 5–10g ginseng to a pot. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 3 minutes. Let cool and enjoy warm or at room temperature.

## Script

This might be the most underrated summer energy drink in traditional Chinese medicine, and it only takes three ingredients to make. In Chinese medicine, summer heat can drain both your energy and your body fluids. That's why so many people feel tired, sluggish, thirsty, and unmotivated when the weather gets hot. One traditional remedy that's been used for generations is mung bean and ginseng tea. Here's how to make it. Add one cup of mung bean, one gallon of water, and five to ten grams of ginseng. Bring it to a boil and let it simmer for just three minutes. After that, let it cool down naturally. Drink it warm or at room temperature throughout the day. In TCM, mung beans are traditionally used to help clear summer heat. Ginseng helps replenish qi, which is the energy that can become depleted from heat, stress, or overwork. Together, they create a refreshing drink that's designed to cool you down while supporting your energy. So if summer leaves you feeling exhausted, this might be the drink that your grandparents would have reached for before energy drinks ever existed.
