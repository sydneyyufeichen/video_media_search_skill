## Script

Did you know in traditional Chinese medicine, even how you dry your clothes can affect your health? That's why in China, people still love to sun dry their laundry, and here's why you should too. In TCM, the sun isn't just light and heat. It carries yang energy, the warm life-giving force that balances out dampness and cold. When you hang your clothes or bedding outside, the sun naturally clears away moisture and invisible dampness that could otherwise cling to the fabric. Dampness in Chinese medicine is one of the biggest hidden causes of fatigue, heaviness, allergies, even joint pain. So when you sleep under bedding that's been dried under the sun, you're not just enjoying the smell. You're literally wrapping yourself in warmth and vitality. Modern science even backs this up. Sunlight kills bacteria and dust mites naturally, giving your body a cleaner, healthier environment to rest in. And think about the mental side too, when you bring in sun-dried clothes or sheets. That fresh, warm scent isn't just pleasant, it actually lifts your mood. So whenever you can, let the sun do the work. Your body, your mind, even your sleep will thank you.
