## Caption

DIY Herbal Tummy Stickers 🌿

Today, we’re making tummy-soothing stickers with just 3 simple ingredients: longan, mugwort leaves, and Sichuan pepper. These are great for relieving bloating, improving digestion, and warming up your stomach! They are also great for diarrhea and insomnia😌

Ingredients:

	•	10g dried longan (龙眼肉)
	•	5g mugwort leaves (艾叶)
	•	3g Sichuan pepper (花椒)
	•	A few pieces of cotton or gauze
	•	Medical tape

How to Make:

1.	Grind the longan, mugwort leaves, and Sichuan pepper into a slightly sticky lump (or chop finely if you don’t have a grinder).

2.	Gently warm the mixture in a pan for 1-2 minutes to activate the herbs’ properties. (Optional)

3.	Place a small amount of the warm mixture onto a piece of cotton or gauze.

4.	Secure the gauze with medical tape onto your tummy, focusing on the area below your navel.

5.	Leave it on for 20-30 minutes (or overnight) while relaxing to let the herbs work their magic.

Pro Tip: Use before bed or during a nap for maximum relaxation. Always patch-test first if you have sensitive skin!
