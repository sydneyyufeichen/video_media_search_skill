Your burps tell us more about your health than you think. Some people have burps non-stop throughout the day. They got the scope done and found nothing wrong. Let me tell you what's going on. From the perspective of traditional Chinese medicine, TCM categorizes burps based on its sound and smell. If the burp has a sour, rotten smell, along with symptoms like bloating or discomfort, it's poor digestion. Eat less or give your stomach a good massage. If you have frequent loud burps and the bloating is often relieved after belching, it's usually emotions. If you have frequent burps without a smell, but with stomach pains, it's often from coldness in your stomach, so stay away from any cold drink or food to start with.
