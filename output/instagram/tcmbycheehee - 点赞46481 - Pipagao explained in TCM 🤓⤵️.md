The main herbs in Ninjong Pipa Gao include Lowquat leaf, which is pipa ye. Lowquat leaf can help clear the heat in our lungs and help stop coughing. Chuan Bei Mu, Fritillaria bowl, is used to moisten dryness and to dissolve phlegm. Platygolden root, which is called jie geng, is used to guide all of the herbs upward so that it can work on our throat and our voice. Honey is used to moisten the yin and to soothe the throat. This syrup also contains more than 10 other ingredients that are also supporting these main ingredients to help with sore throat and to help with our coughing and our voice. If you have dry, tickly coughs, if your voice is straining from overuse, for example, if you talk too much or if you're a public speaker or a singer, or if you have an itchy throat from pollution or air conditioning, for those who are recovering in the post stages of fever or sore throats, this is great for you. Now, this is not suitable for chesty, phlegmy cough or the cold type coughs. If you catch a cold or if you have a chill, it's more moistening than dispersing. So this is not the type of cough medicine you want to use for a cold. So the next time you reach for it, ask yourself, do you have a dry cough or a damp one?
