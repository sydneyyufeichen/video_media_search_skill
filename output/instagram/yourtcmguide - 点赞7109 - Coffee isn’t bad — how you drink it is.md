## Script

Coffee isn't the problem. Drinking coffee on an empty stomach is a problem, and Chinese medicine explains why. Coffee is relatively new to China, only a few hundred years, but the Chinese medicine principles we use to understand the effects of coffee are thousands of years old. In TCM, roasted and bitter substances create internal heat and dryness, and coffee checks both boxes. That heat overstimulates your heart yang, which shows up as anxiety, raising thoughts, palpitations, or poor sleep. At the same time, coffee dries your yin and fluids, which leads to dry mouth, night sweats, hot flashes, and that wired-then-crashed feeling. And here's a rule in Chinese medicine people ignore all the time. Never drink coffee on an empty stomach. We call it shangwei, injuring the stomach. It weakens digestion, worsens acid reflux, and disrupts hormonal balance, especially with PCOS, PMS, or menopause. I still drink coffee, but I always eat first. Protein, bread, something grounding. If you already run hot, coffee on an empty stomach is gasoline on a fire.
