## Script

If you're eating late at night, you're going against one of the oldest longevity rules in Chinese medicine. Let me explain. In ancient Taoist practice, people didn't eat three meals a day. They ate two. Breakfast between 7 to 9 a.m. and lunch between 1 and 3 p.m. After that, nothing. No snacks, no late dinners, no drinks. Just stay hungry. And yeah, it sounds extreme, but the logic is simple. The longer you keep your body slightly hungry, the longer and healthier you live. Now here's where it gets interesting. Modern Western research says something very similar. When you stop eating for long enough, your body runs out of incoming fuel. So your cells start to eat other cells, but not the healthy ones. They go after the weak, damaged, unnecessary ones first. Your body literally starts cleaning itself. So every time you're constantly eating, you're interrupting that process. That's why even today, people try a more realistic version of this. Not stopping at 3 p.m. because life is different now, but cutting everything off after 7 p.m. No late night meals, no random snacking, no constant drinks. Because the moment you keep feeding your body, it never gets the chance to repair itself. So if you actually care about your health, remember this. Stop eating so often, stay a little hungry. That's when your body does its best work.
