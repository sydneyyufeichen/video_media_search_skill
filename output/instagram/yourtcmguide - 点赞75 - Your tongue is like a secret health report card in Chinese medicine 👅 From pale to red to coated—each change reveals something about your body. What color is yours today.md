## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say that your tongue is like a mirror of your health? It reveals what's happening inside your body. A healthy tongue should be pink with a thin white coating. That means your digestion and circulation are running smoothly. Pale tongue, that can mean low energy or blood deficiency. Red tongue, think excess heat or inflammation in the body. Thick yellow coating, your body might be fighting dampness or heat, often linked to digestion issues. And yes, even cracks, teeth marks or dryness all have their meanings too.
