## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say that sugar is one of the biggest hidden causes of chronic inflammation, even if you eat healthy? Because in TCM, sugar doesn't just affect your weight or blood sugar. It weakens your digestion, especially your spleen, the organ responsible for transforming food into energy and keeping your body light, clear, and balanced. When digestion weakens, the body starts producing dampness. Dampness is like internal humidity, heavy, sticky, and hard to move. And here's the part most people don't know. Most chronic inflammation in TCM comes from dampness that gets stuck and then heats up inside the body. If you feel bloated, foggy, swollen, puffy in the face, or tired after meals. If you gain weight easily, retain water, get acne, or joint stiffness, that's dampness being fed by sugar. Sugar slows digestion, traps fluids, and creates that sticky environment where dampness becomes inflammation. That's why the more sugar you eat, the more heavy, inflamed, and low energy your body feels. You don't need to quit sugar forever, but cutting back gives your digestion space to recover. Clear dampness support your spleen, and your whole body will feel lighter, calmer, and less inflamed.
