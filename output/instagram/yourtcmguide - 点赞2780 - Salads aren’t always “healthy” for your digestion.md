## Script

If you love salads but don't feel great after eating them, this is what Chinese medicine would tell you to do. In traditional Chinese medicine, raw foods like salads are cold in nature. And cold doesn't just mean temperature. It means it weakens your digestion over time. Your digestive system is like a pot on a stove. It needs warmth to break down food properly. So when you eat a lot of cold raw foods, you're basically throwing cold ingredients into that pot and turning the heat down. That's why some people feel bloated, tired, or even get loose stools after a salad. But here's the fix. You don't need to give up salads, you just need to balance them. The easiest way? Add ginger. Sprinkle a little ginger powder into your salad dressing or mix it directly into your bowl. In Chinese medicine, ginger helps warm the stomach, support digestion, and counteract the cold nature of raw foods. So instead of cutting salads out completely, you're balancing them.
