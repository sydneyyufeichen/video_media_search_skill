## Script

If you keep waking up between 1 and 3 a.m., traditional Chinese medicine says it's often related to your liver. In TCM, your body follows something called the organ clock, where each organ has a specific time of day or night where it's most active. And the liver's time is between 1 and 3 a.m. During this window, the liver is working to process and purify the blood, regulate the flow of energy in your body, and help you recover from the entire day. But here's the key. This process works best when you're already in deep sleep. That's why in Chinese medicine, it's recommended to be asleep before 11 p.m. If you're still awake late at night or you constantly wake up between 1 and 3 a.m., your liver may not be able to do this repair work properly. In TCM, the liver is extremely important because it stores blood and keeps qi, which is your body's energy, flowing freely. When the liver is off balance, people often experience stress, irritability, mood swings, or poor sleep. That's why the liver is considered one of the most important organs for overall balance and long-term health in Chinese medicine. Protecting your sleep before midnight is one of the simplest ways to support it. Remember, your body does its most important repair work while you're sleeping.
