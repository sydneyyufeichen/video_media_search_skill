## Script

Most people eat their biggest meal at the worst time. Here's why it ruins your health. In Chinese medicine, your digestive system is strongest at midday. That's when your stomach and spleen are at their peak performance, which is why lunch should be your biggest meal. But at night, your body wants to rest and repair. If you load it with a heavy dinner, instead of healing, it spends hours trying to digest. That's why people feel so sluggish, bloated, and can't sleep well. So flip the script, eat more at lunch and less at dinner. Try it for a week and see how different you feel.
