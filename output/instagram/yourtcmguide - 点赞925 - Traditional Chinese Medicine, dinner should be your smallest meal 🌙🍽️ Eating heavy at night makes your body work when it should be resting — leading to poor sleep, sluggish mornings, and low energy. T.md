## Script

Do you know why in traditional Chinese medicine we say that dinner should be the smallest meal of the day? At night, your body is supposed to rest and repair, not spend hours breaking down a heavy meal. When you overload your stomach at dinner, digestion slows, sleep gets disrupted, and you often will wake up feeling bloated or drained. But if you keep your dinner light, food digests more easily, you sleep more deeply, and you wake up with real energy. Try making dinner your lightest meal for just one week, and notice how your sleep and mornings feel different. Give it a try.
