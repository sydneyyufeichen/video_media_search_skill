Western medicine focuses on identifying the exact cause, like pinpointing a virus or bacteria with scans or tests. Chinese medicine, on the other hand, looks at patterns in the body. We read the pulse, we check the tongue, and we identify what patterns are showing up at each stage of the illness. So even though we don't use high-tech tests, we can still diagnose and treat illness based on the body's patterns and restore balance. So in short, Western medicine is great at looking at the cause of illness, and Chinese medicine focuses on the patterns. We can work hand-in-hand or stand-alone, offering a holistic approach for our patients. And that's what makes Chinese medicine so different. We can use the same herb or the same herbal formula for different diseases, because different diseases at different stages may display the same pattern.
