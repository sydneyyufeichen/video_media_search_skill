Why are your lips always super dry? In Chinese medicine, your lips are connected to your spleen, which is part of your digestive system. If your lips are always pale and dry, it's usually a sign that your digestion is weak. People like this often feel tired regardless of how much they sleep, get bloated after meals, and even struggle to concentrate during the day. On the other hand, if your lips are dry, red, or even cracked, it means there's too much heat in your digestive system. You might notice yourself getting angry more easily, tossing and turning at night, or dealing with heartburn or acid reflux. Your lips are like little warning lights on your dashboard. They tell you what's happening inside your body. So what can you do? If your lips are pale and dry, focus on eating warm, easy-to-digest foods, and avoid skipping meals. If they're red and cracked, try cutting back on spicy, greasy foods, and make sure you drink enough water. Your body is always giving you signals. The key is knowing how to read them.
