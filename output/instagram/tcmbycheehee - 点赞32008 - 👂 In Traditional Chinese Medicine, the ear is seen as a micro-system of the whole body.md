## Script

Let me introduce you to my ear massage routine in Chinese medicine. Some people use this to de-puff their face or for a lifting effect, but these are just some of the benefits. So let's do it together. Start by gently pulling on your earlobes about 10 times. Then pull outward and repeat 10 times until you feel a comfortable warmth. Move on to lifting the tips of your ears upward in the same gentle motion. Then, give your ears a light squeeze and fold them 10 times. Place your ear between your index and middle finger and gently rub up and down 10 times. Finally, hold and release your ear a couple of times to finish off. This routine helps activate key acupressure points linked to your body's channels, supporting your overall health and well-being.
