It is made with 7 white herbs crushed into powder:
🌿白术Atractylodes macrocephala
🌿白芍 Paeonia lactiflora
🌿白芨 Bletilla striata
🌿白蔹 Ampelopsis japonica
🌿白薇 Radix cynanchi atrati 
🌿白茯苓 Indian Buead, Tuckahoe
🌿白僵蚕 Bombyx Batryticatus

It can be mixed with water, or egg whites (for oily skin), or honey (for normal skin and dry skin). The powder must be very fine for best results.
