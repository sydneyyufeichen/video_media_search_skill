## Script

Most people think the year only has four seasons, but if you're a real Chinese baddie, you know that there are actually 24. They're called the 24 solar terms, and they divide the year into 24 small seasonal shifts, each lasting about 15 days. These seasons track subtle changes in temperature, sunlight, humidity, and plant life. For thousands of years in China, people used them to know when to plant crops, what foods to eat, how to adjust daily habits, and how to protect their health. It's such an important cultural system that it was even recognized by UNESCO as an intangible cultural heritage of humanity. So instead of just spring, summer, fall, and winter, the year moves through 24 phases of nature. Some have beautiful names like beginning of spring, grain rain, white dew, frost descent. Right now, because we're in mid-March, we're in one called awakening of insects. This is when the weather starts warming quickly, and nature begins waking up after winter. In Chinese medicine, the idea is simple. When nature changes, your body changes too. So instead of living the same way all year, people would adjust their diet, sleep, and lifestyle with each of these 24 seasons. It's basically a calendar around nature, and your health.
