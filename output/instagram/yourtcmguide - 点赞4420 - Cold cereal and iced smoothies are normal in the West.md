## Script

Cold breakfast is normal in the West, but in China, that would never be considered healthy. And here's why. In China, breakfast is almost always served warm. Warm porridge, hot buns, warm soy milk. This isn't just culture, there's a Chinese medicine reason behind it. In TCM, we follow something called the organ clock. It's the idea that your body runs on a daily rhythm, and different organs take turns being the most active throughout the day. According to the organ clock, 7 to 9 a.m. is when your stomach and spleen are strongest. These organs are responsible for digestion and turning food into energy. So this is a time your body is designed to eat and absorb nutrients. This is why so many people feel bloated, foggy, or tired after breakfast. It's not always what you eat, it's how and when you eat. Cold first thing in the morning forces your digestion to work overtime. So when you start your day with cold cereal, ice smoothies, or cold water, you're basically throwing ice on a warm engine. Over time, that weakens digestion and drains your energy. Try this instead. Eat a warm breakfast for 7 days. Warm foods, warm drinks. Then pay attention to your energy, digestion, and focus.
