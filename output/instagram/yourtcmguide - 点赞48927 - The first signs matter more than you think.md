## Script

Stop. If you're just starting to feel a cold coming in, chills, slight headache, stuffy nose, this is the Chinese medicine remedy you should do tonight. In traditional Chinese medicine, the moment you feel those first symptoms is critical. That's when the pathogen is still on the surface, not deep in your body yet. And one of the oldest remedies we use is boiled pear. Not supplements, not pills, just a pear. Cooked. When early cold or flu symptoms appear, your lungs get tight and dry. That's why you feel congestion, chills, and that dull head pressure. Cooked pear gently opens the lungs, moisturizes dryness, and helps your body push the illness out instead of letting it settle in. Take one pear. Asian pear is great, but any pear works. Slice it up and add about a cup of water. Bring it to a boil, then let it simmer for two to three minutes. Drink the warm liquid first, then eat the pear. This is something many of us grew up drinking in Chinese households the second we felt sick. Try it at the first sign, not after you're already down.
