## Script

In China, everyone spits out phlegm because in traditional Chinese medicine, phlegm isn't just gross mucus. It's considered a pathological substance, something your body is actively trying to get rid of. So when you swallow it back down, you're basically sending waste right back inside. In TCM, phlegm comes from something called dampness. It happens when your body can't properly transform fluids. Think of it like this. Your system is supposed to be like a warm functioning kitchen. If things get too cold or sluggish, everything starts to pool, thicken, and stagnate. That's dampness. And if dampness sits for too long, it congeals into phlegm. There are actually two types. First, the obvious one, the mucus you cough up or feel in your throat. But second, this is what most people miss, invisible phlegm. This can show up as brain fog, heaviness in the body, feeling bloated or sluggish, even things like nodules or chronic conditions. So when phlegm rises to your throat, that's not random. That's your body trying to push it out. This is why in Chinese medicine, we don't suppress it. We support the body in clearing it. If you're someone dealing with a lot of phlegm, start by cutting back on things that make it worse. Cold foods, dairy, sugar, fried foods, because all of that just feeds the dampness that your body is trying to clear.
