When you're constantly stressed, anxious, or fearful, your qi can become stagnant, which can affect everything from sleep and digestion to how you feel every day. Researchers have found that when people genuinely believe a treatment will help, they often experience less pain, lower stress, and even measurable changes in their brain chemistry.
