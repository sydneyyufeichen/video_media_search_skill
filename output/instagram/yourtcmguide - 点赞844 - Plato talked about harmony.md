## Script

If you want to understand Chinese medicine, you might have to start with Plato. Plato says something powerful. A person is healthy and just when all parts are in harmony. When nothing is fighting each other, when everything is doing its job the way it's supposed to. Now think about that. That's exactly how traditional Chinese medicine sees your body. In TCM, health isn't random, it's rhythm. Your body runs on something called the organ clock. Every organ has a time when it's strongest, a time to work, repair, and reset. Your stomach has a time to digest, your liver has a time to restore, and your lungs have a time to recover. So the goal isn't to control your body, it's to support it. Because when each organ does its job well, your body becomes healthy. And when your body is healthy, your mind becomes clear, calm, and stable. You can't have a healthy mind without a healthy body. But most people today are completely out of sync. Eating at random times, sleeping too late, constantly interrupting their body's natural rhythm. So their body never gets the chance to work the way it was designed to. And over time, things start to break down. So your job is actually simple. Eat at regular times, sleep at the right time, stop working against your body. Follow the organ clock, and your body starts to work with you, not against you. That's how you build real health, and that's how you build a better mind.
