Why do you need to avoid certain foods when you are taking herbal medication? If you've ever had herbal prescriptions from a Chinese medicine practitioner, they would most likely tell you to avoid certain foods like cold foods, sour foods, seafood. They will tell you to avoid different types of food according to your condition. And that's because food has energetic properties. It has medicinal properties. Food is medicine. For example, if I'm helping you warm your body, but you're taking cold foods like cold drinks, ice drinks, that is canceling out some of the effects from the herbs. So that's why in Chinese medicine, there's a saying, 三分治七分養. So the herbal prescription is 30% of the treatment, but 70% of the treatment relies on your cooperation, be it in your diet, your exercise, your lifestyle. So you can ask your practitioner, how can you recover faster? What foods do you need to avoid and what foods do you need to eat more of to help support the treatment? Because that's the most effective way to support your body and it's also the fastest way for you to recover.
