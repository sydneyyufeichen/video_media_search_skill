## Script

Do you know why in traditional Chinese medicine we say that eating local and in-season produce is like sinking your body to nature's rhythm? In traditional Chinese medicine, your body is seen as a microcosm of nature, like a tiny reflection of the world around you. When the seasons change, your body changes too. Your organs shift their workload, your energy flows differently, even your emotions respond to the weather. Nature actually gives us the exact medicine we need every season. In spring, tender greens like peas and spinach help cleanse and awaken your liver after winter. In summer, juicy fruits like peaches, cucumbers, and melons help cool and hydrate you when the heat builds up. In autumn, grounding foods like sweet potatoes, pumpkins, and squash nourish your lungs and strengthen immunity. And in winter, hearty greens, grains, and warming spices protect your kidneys and build deep energy for the cold months ahead. But when food is shipped across the world, it's picked too early and loses that living seasonal energy before it reaches you. Local seasonal food is fresher, more vibrant, and perfectly aligned with what your body needs right now. So next time you're at the market, ask yourself, what's growing around me right now?
