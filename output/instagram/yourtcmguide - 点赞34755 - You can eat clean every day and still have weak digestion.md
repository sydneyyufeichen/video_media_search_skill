## Script

If you think you're healthy just because you eat clean, that's honestly just level one in China. Because in Chinese medicine, it's not only what you eat, it's how you eat, when you eat, and how your body receives it that determines your gut health. So here are the top three habits that Chinese people avoid to keep their digestion strong. Number one, overeating. In Chinese medicine, food is meant to nourish your qi, your energy. But when you eat past fullness, you overload your stomach and spleen. Over time, that leads to bloating, fatigue, and sluggish digestion. The goal isn't eating until you're stuffed. It's eating until you're comfortably satisfied and stopping there. Number two, drinking cold water. This one shocks a lot of people. Cold drinks weaken stomach tea and slow digestion, especially if you do it every day. That's why many Chinese people avoid ice water altogether and drink warm or room temperature drinks instead. A cold gut means slower digestion, more bloating, and less energy. Three, eating too late at night. Your stomach needs rest just like your brain does. Late night eating forces digestion to work when it should be repairing. Over time, this weakens your gut, your energy levels, and even your immune system. In Chinese medicine, digestion is the foundation of health. When your gut is strong, everything else works better.
