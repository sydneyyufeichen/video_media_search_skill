I have a friend who works in the plastics industry who once told me, if you see black forks at the party, stay away, because they come from the dirtiest recycled material. Studies also show black plastic is often made from recycled electronics. That means it can contain a variety of chemicals linked to cancer and toxic flame retardants. So it's probably e-waste you're licking. Not only black forks at the party, please also pay attention to takeout containers, because it gets worse when there's hot food in there. Heat dramatically increases chemical leaching. We're talking dozens of times faster. Your meal could be absorbing those toxins. According to that friend of mine, it's best to go for disposable plastic products that are clear or light colored. So please share this video to protect someone you care about.
