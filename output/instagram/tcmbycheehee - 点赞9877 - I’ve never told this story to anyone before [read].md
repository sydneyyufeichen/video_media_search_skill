I started studying Chinese medicine because I wanted to find a natural alternative to help my family stay healthy. But never did I expect that in my second year, before I barely learned anything useful, my grandmother passed away right before my eyes.

I recall years earlier when grandpa left us, and we were all by his bedside. I knew then that I wanted to care for my family’s health, but why did this have to happen again?

Heartbroken, I told myself that I will never let this tragedy happen to my family again. I know that I cannot prevent death, but at least I want them to leave this world peacefully without any illness. I studied, studied, and studied. I didn’t want to miss any details, in case I could help someone with the information I learned.

Two months ago, I graduated top of my class as the valedictorian, and I’m currently helping my family with the treatments I’ve learned for the past five years. I pray that my family stays healthy, and I hope this story inspires some of you to walk on the path of medicine.

My mission now is to spread health awareness and tips through social media, because I believe that the root of all illness lies in lack of health awareness and unhealthy lifestyles.
