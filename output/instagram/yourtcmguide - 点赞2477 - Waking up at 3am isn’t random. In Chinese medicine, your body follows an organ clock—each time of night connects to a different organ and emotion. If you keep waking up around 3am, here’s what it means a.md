## Script

If you wake up at 3 a.m., your body is trying to tell you something important. In Chinese medicine, your body follows an organ clock. Between 1 a.m. and 3 a.m., your liver is doing its main night shift, clearing out waste and processing everything from your day. If your liver energy is blocked, maybe from overeating, stress, or alcohol, you might wake up at this time. Between 3 a.m. and 5 a.m., the focus shifts to your lungs. Waking then can be linked to emotions such as sadness or grief, or even breathing issues. If you often wake up around this time, try sleeping earlier. Avoid heavy meals or alcohol at night, and do a quick stretch or breathing exercise before bed. Follow for more Chinese medicine tips you can use every day.
