## Script

The Chinese medicine remedy to hair loss? Two things, black sesame seeds and a scalp massage, literally massaging your own scalp. In traditional Chinese medicine, these two simple habits have been used for thousands of years to support fuller, healthier looking hair because they work from the inside and the outside. Black sesame seeds nourish your kidneys and liver, the organs and TCM that govern blood, essence, and overall hair nourishment. When those are supported, your hair gets stronger from within. They're easy to add into your routine. Sprinkle black sesame on oatmeal, rice, or yogurt, blend it into smoothies, or lightly toast and eat a small spoonful a day. Consistency is what matters. Scalp massage is the external part. Use your fingers, a brush, or an oxhorn comb like I'm using here. The goal is simply to stimulate the scalp, improve circulation, and bring more nourishment to the hair roots. Just one to two minutes a day is enough. In TCM, internal nourishment plus external stimulation is the classic approach. Black sesame strengthens the foundation. Scalp massage delivers it to the roots.
