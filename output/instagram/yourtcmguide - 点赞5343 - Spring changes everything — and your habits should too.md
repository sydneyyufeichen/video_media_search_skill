Spring is finally here. And if you're a real Chinese baddie, here are three things you need to stop doing immediately. Number one, stop sleeping late and waking up late. If you're still staying up till 1am and dragging yourself out of bed, you're literally working against the season. And that's why you feel tired even after enough sleep. Number two, stop eating heavy greasy winter foods every day. Winter is for storing, spring is for moving. Your body wants simple, fresher foods now. Think greens, sprouts, simple meals. If you keep eating heavy like it's January, you'll feel sluggish, bloated, and tired all day. Number three, stop staying stuck indoors. Spring is all about movement and liver qi flowing. If you're not going outside, not stretching, not moving, that energy gets stuck. And when it's stuck, mood swings, irritability, low energy. So if you want to move with the season like a real Chinese baddie, sleep earlier, eat lighter, and move your body more. Because in Chinese medicine, health isn't about fighting nature, it's about flowing with it.
