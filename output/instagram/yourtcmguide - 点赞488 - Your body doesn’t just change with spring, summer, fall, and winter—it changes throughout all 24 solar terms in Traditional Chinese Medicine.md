## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine, we say your body follows 24 seasons instead of just four? Most people think it's just summer right now. But in China, today isn't just summer. We're currently in a solar term called xiao shu, or minor heat, one of the 24 solar terms that divide the year into much smaller seasonal changes. It lasts from July 7th through July 22nd, just before the hottest part of the year, called da shu, or major heat. For thousands of years, Chinese medicine has figured out that your body doesn't just respond to spring, summer, fall, and winter. It responds to subtle changes in temperature, humidity, wind, and sunlight throughout the year. That's why your diet and daily habits are supposed to change every couple of weeks, not just every few months. During xiao shu, the weather becomes hotter and more humid, so Chinese families traditionally eat foods that help clear summer heat, support digestion, and replenish body fluids. The goal isn't to fight nature, it's to stay in balance with it. And that's why in traditional Chinese medicine, there aren't just four seasons. There are 24 opportunities every year to adjust your lifestyle before your body falls out of balance.
