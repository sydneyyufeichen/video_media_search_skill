## Script

If your period is irregular, should you be concerned? The short answer is yes. Let me explain. Your menstrual cycle is like a monthly health report card, and honestly, it's one of the luckiest things about being a woman. When your period shows up consistently around the same time every month, it usually means your body is in balance. For most people, that's around 28 to 31 days, with bleeding lasting 5 to 7 days. But for some, even a shorter cycle can be normal for them. But if your cycle is unpredictable, 25 days one month, 40 the next, or skipping months, that's your body trying to get your attention. In Chinese medicine, a woman's health is rooted in the liver system. Your liver regulates your emotions and energy, stores blood, and keeps everything flowing smoothly. So if you're stressed, not sleeping well, not moving your body, or constantly having cold drinks or cold exposure, that can disrupt your flow over time. And when that flow is disrupted, your cycle becomes irregular. If your cycle has been irregular, don't ignore it. Track it. Pay attention to the patterns. Because your period isn't random. It's feedback.
