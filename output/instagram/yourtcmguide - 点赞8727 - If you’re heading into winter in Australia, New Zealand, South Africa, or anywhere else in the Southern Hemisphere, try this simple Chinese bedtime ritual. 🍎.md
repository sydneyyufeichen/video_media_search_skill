Cut one apple into small pieces. Skin on or off, totally up to you. Boil it in water and let it gently simmer for about 5 minutes. When it's done, drink the warm sweet apple water and eat the soft pieces too. In traditional Chinese medicine, apples are believed to nourish the spleen and stomach, which are responsible for turning food into energy. But here's the interesting part. Raw apples are actually considered cooling. When you boil them, they become much gentler on digestion and better suited for colder weather. That's why boiled apples have long been used as a simple winter remedy in Chinese households. Many people find they help with bloating, support digestion, and relieve some of the dryness that comes with winter air. And because you're drinking it warm before bed, it's also an incredibly comforting way to unwind after a long day. So if winter is arriving where you live, give this a try tonight. One apple, 5 minutes, and a warm cup before bed.
