## Script

Have you ever noticed that people in China drink beer almost room temperature, while Americans want it as cold as physically possible? There's actually a really interesting cultural reason for that. In America, cold beer is seen as more refreshing. The colder it is, the better. But in China, people grow up hearing that cold drinks weaken digestion. In traditional Chinese medicine, your stomach is viewed almost like a cooking pot. It needs warmth to properly break down food and transform it into energy. So when you drink ice cold beer, especially with meals, it's thought to shock the stomach and slow digestion down. That's why in China, beer is often served room temperature, not freezing cold like in the US. And it's not just beer. You'll notice less ice in drinks, more hot tea at restaurants, and people avoiding large amounts of icy drinks altogether. Because from a TCM perspective, too much cold can lead to bloating, fatigue, loose stools, and that heavy sluggish feeling after eating. Now obviously, people in China still enjoy cold beer sometimes too. But culturally, there's a much bigger focus on protecting digestion, even if that means sacrificing a little of the icy refreshing feeling Americans love.
