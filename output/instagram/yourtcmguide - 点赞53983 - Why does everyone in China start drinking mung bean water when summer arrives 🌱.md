## Script

Hey guys, I'm going to tell you why everyone in China starts drinking mung bean water in the summer, and so should you. And no, it's not some new wellness trend. Chinese people have been doing this for hundreds of years. The moment summer arrives, you'll see mung bean water everywhere in China. But why? According to traditional Chinese medicine, summer is associated with heat, and when too much heat builds up in the body, it can show up as acne breakouts, mouth ulcers, irritability, poor sleep, excessive sweating, and feeling exhausted by the afternoon. And that's where mung beans come in. In Chinese medicine, mung beans are famous for clearing heat and helping the body cool down naturally. They're also thought to reduce summer dampness, that heavy, sluggish feeling where you feel tired and bloated during hot weather. That's why so many Chinese families drink mung bean water all summer long, and the recipe is incredibly simple. Just take half a cup of mung bean and add it to six cups of water. Bring it to a boil and let it simmer for about 30 minutes or until the beans start to split and soften. Then drink the water and eat the beans too. So if summer heat has you feeling drained, this might be one of the simplest Chinese medicine remedies worth trying.
