## Script

Do you know why in Chinese medicine we say the shape of your poop is like a health report card? Because that shape can reveal exactly what's going on deep inside your digestion. If your poop is hard and round like little pellets, that usually means too much heat and dryness, your body's dehydrated, or eating too much spicy fried food. If it's thin or narrow like a noodle, that points to stress or tension. In TCM, that's the liver squeezing your digestion too tightly. If it's mushy, loose, or shapeless, that's weak digestion. Your stomach fire isn't strong enough to transform food, so everything passes too quickly. And if it's long, smooth, and well-formed, that's what we call healthy. Your body's fire, fluids, and balance are just right. In Chinese medicine, your stomach and spleen are like a kitchen. When the fire burns evenly and moisture is balanced, food transforms properly. The shape of your poop is the final dish, showing how well your inner system is working. So the next time you go, take a quick look. In Chinese medicine, even the shape of your poop is your body's way of showing you how it's really doing inside.
