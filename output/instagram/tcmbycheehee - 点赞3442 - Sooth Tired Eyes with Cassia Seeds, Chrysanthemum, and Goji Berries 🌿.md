## Caption

Sooth Tired Eyes with Cassia Seeds, Chrysanthemum, and Goji Berries 🌿

In Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), tired or strained eyes are often linked to Liver imbalance or prolonged screen time. These three ingredients are known to support eye health and relieve fatigue.

1. Cassia Seeds (决明子)

🌿 Benefits:

* Clears Liver heat, reducing redness and irritation in the eyes.

* Relieves eye strain caused by excessive screen use or poor sleep.

* Supports digestion and detoxification, which can indirectly improve eye health.

2. Chrysanthemum (菊花)

🌿 Benefits:

* Clears heat and soothes tired, dry, or irritated eyes.

* Known for improving vision and reducing discomfort caused by long hours of reading or computer use.

* Nourishes the Liver, which is closely linked to eye health in TCM.

3. Goji Berries (枸杞)

🌿 Benefits:

* Rich in carotenoids and antioxidants that protect and nourish the eyes.

* Improves vision and reduces dryness, especially for those who work in front of screens.

* Strengthens the Liver and Kidney, key organs in maintaining eye health in TCM.

Eye-Relief Tea Recipe

Ingredients:

* 1 tablespoon cassia seeds (决明子)

* 5–6 chrysanthemum flowers (菊花)

* 1 tablespoon goji berries (枸杞)

Instructions:

1. Slightly toast cassia seeds for a nutty aroma (optional).

2. Add cassia seeds, chrysanthemum flowers, and goji berries to a teapot.

3. Pour in 2–3 cups of hot water and steep for 10 minutes.

4. Strain and enjoy warm.

Bonus Tip:

* Pair this tea with regular breaks from screens and eye exercises to further reduce strain.

* Avoid cassia seeds if you have loose stools, as they have a mild laxative effect.

Keep your eyes refreshed and nourished with this simple yet effective remedy! 🌼👀✨
