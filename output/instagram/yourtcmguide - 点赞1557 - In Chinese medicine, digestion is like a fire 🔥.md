Hey guys, I'm going to tell you why everyone in China eats warm, cooked breakfast and why your cold smoothie every morning might be a mistake. In traditional Chinese medicine, your stomach is like a fire. And in the morning, that fire is still weak and slowly waking up. So when the first thing you consume is iced coffee, cold yogurt, smoothies, or raw fruit, it's like pouring ice water onto a small fire. Your body now has to use extra energy to warm everything up before you can digest it. That's why traditional Chinese breakfasts are usually warm and cooked. Think congee, soups, steamed buns, eggs, warm soy milk. Because warm foods are easier on digestion and easier for your body to turn into energy. That's also why many people in China say cold breakfast can leave you feeling sluggish, bloated, foggy, or hungry again an hour later. And honestly, a lot of people notice they feel less bloated, more energized, and more satisfied throughout the morning when they switch to warm, cooked breakfast. In Chinese medicine, breakfast is supposed to strengthen your body, not shock it awake.
