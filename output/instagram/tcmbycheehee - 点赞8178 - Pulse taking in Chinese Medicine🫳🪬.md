## Script

Did you know that your pulse can reveal the health of your organs? Well, in traditional Chinese medicine, we don't just check if our pulse is fast or slow. We feel for three different positions on each wrist, and each one connects to a different organ. Your left wrist reflects your heart, liver, and kidney yin. A strong wiry pulse here means that you might have liver qi stagnation or you're stressed. And a weak pulse usually means that you have a weak heart qi, causing dizziness or tiredness. Your right wrist reflects your lungs, your spleen, and your kidney yang. If you have a slippery pulse here, it can mean that you have a weak digestion or you have some dampness in your body. A deep, slow pulse might mean that your body has low metabolism, is too cold, and has low energy levels. And did you know that in traditional Chinese medicine, we have more than 24 different types of pulses? By feeling the pulse at different depths, we can understand not just how your organs are working, but also check if your body is at a balance. It's like a full body checkup without any machines.
