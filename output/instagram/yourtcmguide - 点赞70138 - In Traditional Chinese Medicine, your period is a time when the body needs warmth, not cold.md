## Script

Do you know why women in China avoid cold and raw foods during their period? It's not a superstition. It's one of the most important principles in traditional Chinese medicine. In TCM, your period is a time when your body is naturally more open and vulnerable. Your uterus is shedding, your circulation is working harder, and your body needs warmth to keep everything moving smoothly. Cold foods like ice drinks, smoothies, salads, and sushi introduce cold energy into the body. And when your system is already under stress, that cold can actually slow down the flow of blood. When the flow slows, you get exactly what so many women complain about. Stronger cramps, more clots, lower back pain, bloating, and even fatigue. It's the same idea as muscles tightening up in cold weather, but this time it's your uterus and the vessels around it responding to temperature. That's why warm cooked foods are preferred. Soups, stews, ginger tea, things that support circulation and keep the body relaxed instead of constricted. And here's the interesting part. Women who switch to warm foods during their cycle report less cramping, better flow, and more stable moods, sometimes within one or two cycles. So the idea isn't don't eat cold food. It's during your period, keep your body warm, and your body will take better care of you.
